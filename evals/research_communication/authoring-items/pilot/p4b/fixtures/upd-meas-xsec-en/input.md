# Talk outline: Fiducial cross section of pp → Xγ (SYNTHETIC)

> All numbers in this file are synthetic and were made up for an exercise. They do not correspond to any real measurement.

Slot: 15 min + 5 min questions. Status of the result: **Preliminary**.

---

## Slide 1 — Title

- "Measurement of the fiducial cross section of pp → X(→ℓℓ)γ at √s = 13.6 TeV"
- Labels on slide: **PRELIMINARY**, **Synthetic data**

## Slide 2 — Motivation

- Xγ production probes the trilinear coupling structure of the (synthetic) X boson.
- First measurement in this fiducial region.

## Slide 3 — Dataset and selection

- √s = 13.6 TeV, integrated luminosity L = 140 fb⁻¹.
- Two opposite-charge same-flavour leptons, one isolated photon with E_T > 30 GeV.
- Observed events: N_obs = 1462.
- Estimated background: N_bkg = 1050 ± 40.
- Signal yield: N_sig = 412 ± 31 (stat).

## Slide 4 — Result

- Fiducial acceptance × efficiency from simulation with data-driven trigger scale factors: εA = 0.236.
- σ_fid = N_sig / (εA · L)
- **σ_fid = 12.5 ± 0.9 (stat) ± 0.7 (syst) fb**

## Slide 5 — Comparison with theory

- Figure 2: measured value vs NLO QCD prediction.
- Caption (Fig. 2): "Measured fiducial cross section σ_fid = 12.5 fb (black point, inner bar stat., outer bar total) compared with the NLO QCD prediction 11.0 ± 0.6 fb (blue band). Ratio σ_fid/σ_NLO = 1.14 ± 0.12. Preliminary, synthetic data."
- Body text: "Mild excess over the prediction; difference Δσ = 1.5 fb, corresponding to 1.2σ (measurement and theory uncertainties combined in quadrature)."
- Speaker notes: "Stress that we are about 14% above the prediction but only 1.2σ away, so this is not significant. Do not call it a tension."

## Slide 6 — Limitations

- Fiducial result only; no extrapolation to the full phase space.
- εA relies on simulation corrected with trigger scale factors from 2022 tag-and-probe; dependence on the photon E_T spectrum in simulation not yet assessed beyond the stated systematic.
- Theory prediction is NLO QCD only; NLO electroweak corrections not included.

## Slide 7 — Summary

- σ_fid = 12.5 ± 1.1 (total) fb, measured with 138 fb⁻¹ at √s = 13.6 TeV.
- σ_fid/σ_NLO = 1.14 ± 0.12: consistent with NLO QCD within 1.2σ.
- Result is **preliminary**; synthetic data.
