# Talk outline: Recast of a dijet resonance search for a leptophobic Z′ (SYNTHETIC)

> All numbers in this file are synthetic and made up for an exercise. This is not an official result of any experiment.

Slot: 15 min + 5 min questions. Status: **Phenomenological recast, not an official experimental result**.

---

## Slide 1 — Title

- "Constraints on a leptophobic Z′ from a recast dijet search"
- Labels on slide: **Synthetic**, **Not an official experimental result**

## Slide 2 — Model

- Leptophobic Z′ with universal quark coupling g_q; dark-sector decays switched off.
- Width Γ/m below 10% for the couplings shown.

## Slide 3 — Recast setup

- Uses the public 2023 dataset only: √s = 13.6 TeV, L = 48.3 ± 1.0 fb⁻¹.
- Selection reproduced: two jets with |η| < 2.4, |Δη_jj| < 1.3, m_jj > 1.5 TeV.
- Detector response from a parametrised simulation tuned to the published jet resolution.

## Slide 4 — Validation

- Published benchmark cutflow reproduced within 6% at every step.
- Signal efficiency validated against published values for 1.5–4 TeV.

## Slide 5 — Result

- **Excluded at 95% CL: m_Z′ < 3.2 TeV for g_q = 0.25.**
- Figure 4 caption: "Observed 95% CL upper limit on g_q as a function of m_Z′ from the recast (solid line); excluded region shaded. 2023 dataset, 48.3 fb⁻¹. Synthetic, not an official result."

## Slide 6 — Limitations

- No unfolding: the recast relies on the parametrised detector response.
- Signal efficiency validated only up to 4 TeV; limits above 4 TeV are extrapolations.
- Valid only for Γ/m < 10%; wider resonances are not covered.
- Background fit function taken as published; no independent check.

## Slide 7 — Summary

- Leptophobic Z′ with g_q = 0.25 excluded below 3.2 TeV (95% CL).
- Recast validated to within 6% on the published cutflow.
- Synthetic; not an official experimental result.
