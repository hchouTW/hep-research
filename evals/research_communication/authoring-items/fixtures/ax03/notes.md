# Notes for a phenomenology paper (synthetic; not a real calculation)

- Process: inclusive Z+jet production at sqrt(s) = 13.6 TeV, jet pT > 30 GeV.
- We computed the NLO QCD correction. K-factor K = 1.18, scale variation (mu_R, mu_F varied by factors 2 and 1/2,
  7-point) gives +0.06 / -0.05 on K.
- PDF set: a single NNLO PDF set; PDF uncertainty not yet evaluated.
- Below jet pT ~ 30 GeV, large logarithms of pT/m_Z appear and fixed order is not reliable; resummation is not
  included, so we do not give predictions there.
- Electroweak corrections not included; expected to matter above pT ~ 1 TeV (literature estimate, to be cited).
- The ratio of the Z+jet to the W+jet cross section has scale uncertainty reduced to about 1% because the
  scale dependence largely cancels; the cancellation assumes the same scale choice for both processes.
- Implication we want to argue: the ratio is a useful precision observable for the current LHC run.
