# Prose examples

Illustrative: the experiments, fits, records and numbers are invented and name no collaboration or published analysis.
Each example is in the Contrast format of `docs/dev/example-authoring.md` (scenario, a realistic weak version, a
better version with the reasoning and checks behind it, takeaways) and states its limits. Material: synthetic.

| File | Case | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| [01-contrast-results-paragraph.md](01-contrast-results-paragraph.md) | a preliminary 2.3 sigma spectral break written up as a "first observation" | keep the hedges that state a real limitation with its size, match significance words to the number, keep values and the `preliminary` label from the record |
| [02-contrast-review-synthesis.md](02-contrast-review-synthesis.md) | three EFT fits averaged into a "consensus", and a referee reply claiming a check that was not done | synthesize by the question and the comparison conditions, label the author's judgement, do not average unlike quantities, route the referee point (owner skill or hep-review) and reply from the change ledger |
| [03-contrast-review-talk.md](03-contrast-review-talk.md) | a review talk centred on one study with an invented average | the synthesis is the central claim, the explaining condition survives every cut, `check_adaptation.py` catches the average and the dropped condition (real reports quoted) |
