# Systematic-review screening

Use when the user requests a systematic/scoping review or an auditable screening
process. For ordinary related work, use `literature-review.md`; do not describe
a convenience sample as exhaustive or systematic.

## Define and preserve the protocol

Specify the question, eligible study designs, domain/population, methods or
comparators, outcomes, dates, languages, and treatment of preprints and unpublished
work. Use a domain-appropriate question framework rather than forcing clinical
criteria onto physics or ML. Record criteria before screening; label subsequent
changes and revisit affected decisions. Confirm consequential missing criteria
with the user; useful search preparation can proceed meanwhile.

## Search and reconcile records

- Record each database/interface, exact query and filters, search date, coverage
  limits, and returned/exported counts. Save raw exports when available. Log
  backward/forward citation searches separately. Disclose inaccessible databases,
  truncation, and incomplete retrieval; never invent a search or its count.
- Assign stable record IDs and retain provenance. Deduplicate by identifiers,
  then review title/author/year matches. Link preprints and published versions;
  distinguish multiple reports of one study from independent studies so synthesis
  does not count the same evidence twice.
- Track records retrieved, duplicates removed, records screened, reports sought,
  reports unavailable, full texts assessed, exclusions, and included reports and
  studies. Reconcile counts from the decision log, preserving the distinction
  between records, reports, and studies.

## Screen with traceable decisions

Screen title/abstract first, then full text. At either stage use include, exclude,
or uncertain with a criterion-based reason and evidence locator. Missing full text
is a retrieval limitation, not proof of ineligibility. Resolve uncertain cases
from additional evidence or flag them for user review.

Keep a ledger: record/study ID, citation/version, source, screening stage, decision,
criterion/reason, evidence locator, reviewer, date, and conflict resolution.
For full-text exclusions use one primary reason for count reconciliation, with
additional reasons in notes. Do not claim independent dual screening unless two
independent reviewers actually screened the material; disclose AI-assisted work.

Separate eligibility from study-quality or risk-of-bias assessment. Choose any
formal appraisal tool for the actual study design and verify its current guidance.
If the user requests a reporting standard such as PRISMA, consult its official
current guidance and applicable extension before claiming compliance.

Deliver the protocol/search log, deduplicated decision ledger, reconciled flow
counts, included-study matrix, and unresolved cases. Synthesize only the evidence
actually obtained and state coverage limitations. Do not pool incompatible results
or start a meta-analysis merely because screening is complete.
