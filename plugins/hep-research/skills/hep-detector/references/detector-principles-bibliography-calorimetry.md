# References: calorimeter cluster reconstruction

A split file of the annotated [bibliography](detector-principles-bibliography.md), in the same citation format: the sources of `calorimeter-cluster-reconstruction.md`. Entries that name an experiment or apparatus sit inside example blocks.

## Calorimeter cluster reconstruction

- Particle Data Group (S. Navas et al.), "Particle detectors at accelerators", in Review of Particle Physics, Phys. Rev. D 110 (2024) 030001 and the 2025 update, <https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-particle-detectors-accel.pdf> (read 2026-11-20). Sec. 35.10.2: position resolution as a stochastic and a constant term; pointing from longitudinal segmentation or a preshower ([calorimeter cluster reconstruction](calorimeter-cluster-reconstruction.md)).
- S. Lee, M. Livan and R. Wigmans, "Dual-readout calorimetry", Rev. Mod. Phys. 90 (2018) 025002, DOI 10.1103/RevModPhys.90.025002, arXiv:1712.05494 (read 2026-11-20). Two-signal equations and the event-by-event electromagnetic fraction (sec. V, eq. 6); first prototype for a thin space calorimeter (sec. IV); proposed collider use (sec. I).

- G. Aad et al. (ATLAS Collaboration), "Topological cell clustering in the ATLAS calorimeters and its performance in LHC Run 1", Eur. Phys. J. C 77 (2017) 490, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-017-5004-5, arXiv:1603.02934 (read 2026-11-20). Cell noise fixed per run period, overlap noise included (sec. 2.2.2, eq. 1); noise-significance seed, growth and boundary thresholds (sec. 3.1, eqs. 2-5); splitting at local maxima and shared-cell weights (sec. 3.1.3, eqs. 6-8).
- ATLAS Collaboration, "Electron and photon performance measurements with the ATLAS detector using the 2015-2017 LHC proton-proton collision data", JINST 14 (2019) P12006, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/14/12/P12006, arXiv:1908.00005 (read 2026-11-20). Fixed versus dynamic clusters, converted-photon definition (sec. 4); ambiguity flag (sec. 4.4); first-layer strips against neutral pions (sec. 7.1).
- A. M. Sirunyan et al. (CMS Collaboration), "Electron and photon reconstruction and identification with the CMS experiment at the CERN LHC", JINST 16 (2021) P05014, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/16/05/P05014, arXiv:2012.06888 (read 2026-11-20). Superclustering and refinement (secs. 4.2, 4.4); energy regression (sec. 6.1); log-weighted second moment and preshower spread (sec. 7.1, eq. 7).
- CMS Collaboration, "Energy calibration and resolution of the CMS electromagnetic calorimeter in pp collisions at sqrt(s) = 7 TeV", JINST 8 (2013) P09009, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/8/09/P09009, arXiv:1306.2016 (read 2026-11-20). Preshower plane weights and MIP calibration (sec. 4.3, eq. 4).
- J. Colas et al., "Position resolution and particle identification with the ATLAS EM calorimeter", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 550 (2005) 96, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2005.05.041, arXiv:physics/0505127 (read 2026-11-20). Barycentre bias, its energy and direction dependence, and logarithmic weights per compartment (sec. 3.1, eqs. 2-3); pointing from two compartments (sec. 3.2, eqs. 7-8).
- V. A. Batarin et al., "Precision measurement of energy and position resolutions of the BTeV electromagnetic calorimeter prototype", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 510 (2003) 248, DOI 10.1016/S0168-9002(03)01862-X, arXiv:hep-ex/0209055 (read 2026-11-20). S-curve from drift chambers, its energy and angle dependence, resolution at inclined incidence (sec. 8, eqs. 2-5).
- H. Abramowicz et al., "Performance and Moliere radius measurements using a compact prototype of LumiCal in an electron test beam", Eur. Phys. J. C 79 (2019) 579, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-019-7077-9, arXiv:1812.11426 (read 2026-11-20). Logarithmic weights with a tuned W0 (sec. 7.1, eq. 5).
- W. Atwood et al., "Pass 8: Toward the full realization of the Fermi-LAT scientific potential", 4th Fermi Symposium, eConf C121028 (2012), arXiv:1303.3514 (read 2026-11-20; no DOI recorded). Minimum-spanning-tree clustering against ghost signals (sec. 2.2.1).
- M. Ackermann et al., "The Fermi Large Area Telescope on orbit: event classification, instrument response functions, and calibration", Astrophys. J. Suppl. 203 (2012) 4, DOI 10.1088/0067-0049/203/1/4, arXiv:1206.1896 (read 2026-11-20). Imaging CsI calorimeter (sec. 2.1.2); principal-axis analysis, tracker energy, three energy estimators (sec. 3.2.1).
- G. Ambrosi et al. (DAMPE Collaboration), "Direct detection of a break in the teraelectronvolt cosmic-ray spectrum of electrons and positrons", Nature 552 (2017) 63-66, DOI 10.1038/nature24475, arXiv:1711.10981 (read 2026-11-20). Per-layer RMS and the combined electron-proton shape variable (Methods, eqs. 1-2).
- C. Yue et al., "A parameterized energy correction method for electromagnetic showers in BGO-ECAL of DAMPE", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 856 (2017) 11-16, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2017.03.013, arXiv:1703.02821 (read 2026-11-20). Gap, upstream and rear-leakage corrections; gamma-function profile fit (sec. III, eqs. 1-3).
- L. Wu et al., "Calibration and status of the 3D imaging calorimeter of DAMPE for cosmic ray physics on orbit", IEEE Trans. Nucl. Sci. 65 (2018) 2007, DOI 10.1109/TNS.2018.2814619, arXiv:1901.00734 (read 2026-11-20). Orthogonal-bar imaging in two views (sec. III.A).
- CALICE Collaboration, "Response of the CALICE Si-W electromagnetic calorimeter physics prototype to electrons", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 608 (2009) 372-383, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2009.07.026, arXiv:0811.2354 (read 2026-11-20). Granularity as a particle-flow requirement (sec. 1).
- K. Shpak (for the CALICE Collaboration), "Separation of two electromagnetic or electromagnetic - hadronic showers in CALICE SiW ECAL and ILD", LCWS2017 proceedings, arXiv:1802.00672 (read 2026-11-20; no DOI recorded). Large `lambda_int / X0` of tungsten for longitudinal separation (sec. I, p. 2); two-shower separation by event mixing of test-beam showers (secs. II-III).
- S. Lee et al., "Hadron detection with a dual-readout fiber calorimeter", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 866 (2017) 76, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2017.05.025, arXiv:1703.09120 (read 2026-11-20). Dual-readout energy from the two signals (sec. 3.1, eqs. 1-2).
- N. Akopov et al., "The HERMES dual-radiator ring imaging Cerenkov detector", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 479 (2002) 511-530, DOI 10.1016/S0168-9002(01)00932-9, arXiv:physics/0104033 (read 2026-11-20). Lepton identification with calorimeter, preshower and TRD (p. 13, footnote 3).
