# References: calorimeter cluster reconstruction

A split file of the annotated [bibliography](detector-principles-bibliography.md), in the same citation format: the sources of `calorimeter-cluster-reconstruction.md` and of the hadronic calorimeter pages (`hadronic-calorimeter-reconstruction.md`, `hadronic-calorimeter-timing-and-model-validation.md`). Entries that name an experiment or apparatus sit inside example blocks.

## Calorimeter cluster reconstruction

- Particle Data Group (S. Navas et al.), "Particle detectors at accelerators", in Review of Particle Physics, Phys. Rev. D 110 (2024) 030001, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.110.030001, and the 2025 update, <https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-particle-detectors-accel.pdf> (read 2026-11-20). Sec. 35.10.2: position resolution as a stochastic and a constant term; pointing from longitudinal segmentation or a preshower ([calorimeter cluster reconstruction](calorimeter-cluster-reconstruction.md)).
- S. Lee, M. Livan and R. Wigmans, "Dual-readout calorimetry", Rev. Mod. Phys. 90 (2018) 025002, DOI 10.1103/RevModPhys.90.025002, arXiv:1712.05494 (read 2026-11-20). Two-signal equations and the event-by-event electromagnetic fraction (sec. V, eq. 6); first prototype for a thin space calorimeter (sec. IV); proposed collider use (sec. I).
