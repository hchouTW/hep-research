# References: in-situ calibration and alignment

A split file of the annotated [bibliography](detector-principles-bibliography.md), in the same citation format: the sources of `in-situ-calibration-methods.md` and `in-situ-alignment-and-momentum-scale.md`. Entries that name an experiment or apparatus sit inside example blocks.

## In-situ calibration and alignment

- <!-- example: source citation -->V. Blobel, "A new method for the high-precision alignment of track detectors", arXiv preprint (2002),
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0208021>. Global and local parameters in one least-squares fit (Millepede), bias of
  iterated residual corrections, Lagrange constraints, unconstrained global modes (secs. 1-3). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->V. Eckardt et al., "Calibration of the STAR Forward Time
  Projection Chamber with Krypton-83m", arXiv preprint (2001), <https://arxiv.org/abs/nucl-ex/0101013>. Pulser
  electronics gain versus gas gain, 83mKr gain and energy calibration of a TPC (sec. 1). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->CMS Collaboration, "Energy calibration and resolution of the CMS electromagnetic
  calorimeter in pp collisions at sqrt(s) = 7 TeV", JINST 8 (2013) P09009, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/8/09/P09009.
  Phi-symmetry, pi0/eta and E/p intercalibration, absolute scale from Z decays, systematic floors, combination
  assuming independent methods (secs. 4 and 5.4). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->CMS Collaboration, "Alignment of the CMS tracker with LHC and cosmic ray data",
  JINST 9 (2014) P06009, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/9/06/P06009. Millepede-II block reduction, weak modes
  (sec. 4). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->CMS Collaboration, "Alignment of the CMS muon system with cosmic-ray and beam-halo
  muons", JINST 5 (2010) T03020, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/5/03/T03020. Track-based chamber alignment
  through material, photogrammetry constraints and cross-checks (secs. 1, 3). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->CMS Collaboration, "Performance of CMS muon reconstruction in pp collision events
  at sqrt(s) = 7 TeV", JINST 7 (2012) P10002, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/7/10/P10002. Momentum scale
  from J/psi and Z mass constraints at low and intermediate pT, and from cosmic muons (cosmics endpoint) at high pT
  (abstract; secs. 6, 6.2.2). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->ATLAS Collaboration, "Alignment of the ATLAS Inner Detector in Run-2", Eur. Phys.
  J. C 80 (2020) 1194, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-020-08700-6. Weak modes controlled by track-parameter
  constraints (sec. 3.2.4). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->ATLAS Collaboration, "Single hadron response measurement and calorimeter jet
  energy scale uncertainty with the ATLAS detector at the LHC", Eur. Phys. J. C 73 (2013) 2305,
  DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-013-2305-1. Single-hadron E/p as an in-situ reference, neutral-background subtraction, test of the isolation criterion
  (secs. 1, 3.5, 4.5, 5.1). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->ATLAS Collaboration, "Operation and performance of the ATLAS Tile Calorimeter in
  Run 1", Eur. Phys. J. C 78 (2018) 987, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-018-6374-z. Radioactive source
  scans, test-beam scale transfer, muon response (secs. 4.1, 6.1). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->ATLAS Collaboration, "Muon reconstruction performance of the ATLAS detector in
  proton-proton collision data at sqrt(s) = 13 TeV", Eur. Phys. J. C 76 (2016) 292,
  DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-016-4120-y. Momentum scale from J/psi and Z decays (abstract). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->ALICE Collaboration, "The ALICE Transition Radiation Detector: construction,
  operation, and performance", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 881 (2018) 88, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2017.09.028.
  Pad-by-pad gain calibration with 83mKr (sec. 10.2). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->M. Adinolfi et al., "Performance of the LHCb RICH detector at the
  LHC", Eur. Phys. J. C 73 (2013) 2431, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-013-2431-9. Mirror alignment from
  the Cherenkov-angle residual versus azimuth, refractive-index calibration, track-independent photodetector
  alignment and mirror and gas monitoring (secs. 3.3-3.5). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->G. Anzivino et al., "Precise mirror alignment and basic performance of the RICH detector of the NA62 experiment
  at CERN", JINST 13 (2018) P07012, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/13/07/P07012. Ring-centre offsets per mirror relative to
  a reference mirror (sec. 3.1). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->Fermi-LAT Collaboration, "The on-orbit calibrations for the Fermi Large Area
  Telescope", Astropart. Phys. 32 (2009) 193, DOI 10.1016/j.astropartphys.2009.08.002. Crystal
  scales from protons and nuclei, light asymmetry, tracker alignment and its convergence criterion (secs. 5, 10). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->A. Tykhonov et al. (DAMPE Collaboration), "Internal alignment and position
  resolution of the silicon tracker of DAMPE determined with orbit data", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 893 (2018) 43-56,
  DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2018.02.105. Gradient-descent global alignment, step-size control, temperature
  and humidity dependence (secs. 5, 7). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->L. Wu et al., "Calibration and status of the 3D imaging calorimeter of DAMPE for
  cosmic ray physics on orbit", IEEE Trans. Nucl. Sci. 65 (2018) 2007, DOI 10.1109/TNS.2018.2814619.
  MIP calibration on orbit with protons selected by the geomagnetic cutoff (sec. IV). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->P. v. Doetinchem et al., "Performance of the AMS-02 Transition Radiation
  Detector", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 558 (2006) 526-535, <https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0608641> (arXiv version
  read). Muon pulse-height intercalibration per straw and gas-density correction, beam test
  (sec. 4.2). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->MINOS Collaboration, "The magnetized steel and scintillator calorimeters of the MINOS
  experiment", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 596 (2008) 190-228, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2008.08.003. Multiplicative
  calibration chain, calibration-detector scale transfer, range versus curvature (secs. 2.4.1, 5). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->C. Adloff et al. (CALICE Collaboration), "Hadronic energy resolution of a highly granular scintillator-steel
  hadron calorimeter using software compensation techniques", JINST 7 (2012) P09017,
  DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/7/09/P09017. MIP cell-to-cell calibration in a test beam (sec. 2.2). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->E. P. Segarra et al., "The CLAS12 Backward Angle Neutron Detector (BAND)", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 978 (2020)
  164356, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2020.164356 (arXiv version read). Time walk, effective light speed and attenuation
  length of two-ended bars (secs. 3.1.3-3.1.5). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->K. Abe et al., "Calibration of the
  Super-Kamiokande detector", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 737 (2014) 253-272, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2013.11.081.
  Per-channel time-versus-charge maps from a laser diffuser (sec. 3.1.8). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->D. Dannheim et al., "Corryvreckan: a modular 4D track reconstruction and analysis software for test beam data",
  JINST 16 (2021) P03008, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/16/03/P03008. Reference plane, prealignment, iterative or
  Millepede-II alignment, GBL tracks (secs. 2.3.2, 3.3, 3.5). <!-- /example -->
- <!-- example: source citation -->C. Kleinwort, "General Broken Lines as advanced track fitting method", Nucl. Instrum.
  Methods A 673 (2012) 107-110, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2012.01.024 (arXiv:1201.4320v1 read). Broken-lines track refit
  with kinks at scatterers, solved in time linear in the number of measurements with the full covariance (abstract,
  sec. 1). <!-- /example -->
- Particle Data Group (S. Navas et al.), "Particle detectors for non-accelerator physics", Review of Particle Physics,
  Phys. Rev. D 110 (2024) 030001 and 2025 update (rev. October 2025),
  <https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-particle-detectors-non-accel.pdf>. Optical-module and acoustic
  positioning of large-volume detectors, Moon-shadow pointing check (sec. 36.3.2). Also in the main bibliography.
