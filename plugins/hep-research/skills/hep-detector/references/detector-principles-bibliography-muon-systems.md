# References: muon systems

A split file of the annotated [bibliography](detector-principles-bibliography.md), in the same citation format: the sources of `muon-systems.md` and `non-collider-muon-detection.md`. Entries that name an experiment or apparatus sit inside example blocks.

## Muon systems

- **Particle Data Group reviews**: S. Navas et al. (Particle Data Group), Review of Particle Physics, Phys. Rev. D
  110 (2024) 030001, and the 2025 web update, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.110.030001. *Passage of particles through
  matter* (<https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-passage-particles-matter.pdf>): muon energy loss, critical
  energy and mean range (sec. 34.6, eqs. 34.39-34.40). *Particle detectors at accelerators*
  (<https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-particle-detectors-accel.pdf>): RPC applications (sec. 35.6.6.1),
  sagitta in a solenoid (sec. 35.11, eq. 35.57) and momentum measurement in a uniform field (sec. 35.12).
  *Particle detectors for non-accelerator physics*
  (<https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-particle-detectors-non-accel.pdf>): muon rings of Cherenkov
  telescopes (sec. 36.2.2), muon vetoes of underground detectors (sec. 36.3.1), cosmogenic backgrounds and their
  mitigation (sec. 36.6.5).

- ALICE Collaboration, "Study of cosmic ray events with high muon multiplicity using the ALICE detector at the CERN
  Large Hadron Collider", J. Cosmol. Astropart. Phys. 01 (2016) 032, DOI 10.1088/1475-7516/2016/01/032,
  arXiv:1507.07577v3. Matching up and down tracks of each cosmic muon against double counting (sec. 3).
- ATLAS Collaboration, "Electron and photon performance measurements with the ATLAS detector using the 2015-2017
  LHC proton-proton collision data", J. Instrum. 14 (2019) P12006, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/14/12/P12006,
  arXiv:1908.00005v2. Per-leg electron charge-misidentification rates fitted to same-sign Z -> ee pairs, with the
  same-sign background in the expectation, the mass window as a systematic and a truth closure (sec. 9.2).
- ATLAS Collaboration, "Muon reconstruction performance of the ATLAS detector in proton-proton collision data at
  sqrt(s) = 13 TeV", Eur. Phys. J. C 76 (2016) 292, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-016-4120-y, arXiv:1603.05598v2.
  Calorimeter energy loss from a parametrization combined with the measured energy (sec. 3.2); momentum-scale terms
  (sec. 8.1, eq. 5).
- Borexino Collaboration, "Muon and cosmogenic neutron detection in Borexino", J. Instrum. 6 (2011) P05005,
  DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/6/05/P05005, arXiv:1101.3101v2. Outer water-Cherenkov veto; direct and mutual veto
  efficiencies (sec. 3.2); veto restricted to a cylinder around the muon track (sec. 4).
- CMS Collaboration, "Alignment of the CMS muon system with cosmic-ray and beam-halo muons", J. Instrum. 5 (2010)
  T03020, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/5/03/T03020, arXiv:0911.4022v2. Track-based chamber alignment with photogrammetry
  and in-situ sensors (sec. 1).
- CMS Collaboration, "Performance of CMS muon reconstruction in cosmic-ray events", J. Instrum. 5 (2010) T03022,
  DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/5/03/T03022, arXiv:0911.4994v2. Charge misassignment from the two halves of a cosmic muon
  (sec. 7).
- CMS Collaboration, "Performance of CMS muon reconstruction in pp collision events at sqrt(s) = 7 TeV", J. Instrum.
  7 (2012) P10002, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/7/10/P10002, arXiv:1206.4071v2. Global fit used only above a momentum
  threshold and when its q/p agrees with the tracker-only fit (sec. 3); tag-and-probe on J/psi and Z dimuons with a
  simultaneous pass/fail fit (sec. 5.1); momentum resolution from split cosmic muons, barrel only (sec. 6.2.1).
- CMS Collaboration, "Alignment of the CMS tracker with LHC and cosmic ray data", J. Instrum. 9 (2014) P06009,
  DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/9/06/P06009, arXiv:1403.2286v2. Weak modes that leave the residuals nearly unchanged; twist
  mode biasing curvature in proportion to charge (sec. 4.4).
- S. Corrodi et al., "Performance studies of the Mu2e cosmic ray veto detector", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 1083
  (2026) 171083, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2025.171083, arXiv:2510.13798v1. Three-of-four layer coincidence, defining-module
  efficiency measurement and correlated layer inefficiencies (sec. 6).
- Daya Bay Collaboration, "The muon system of the Daya Bay Reactor antineutrino experiment", Nucl. Instrum. Methods
  A 773 (2015) 8, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2014.09.070, arXiv:1407.0275v3. Water-shield efficiency for muons tagged by an
  inner detector; apparent inefficiency from rock neutrons (sec. 4.3).
- KamLAND Collaboration, "Production of radioactive isotopes through cosmic muon spallation in KamLAND", Phys. Rev.
  C 81 (2010) 025807, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevC.81.025807, arXiv:0907.0066v2. Distance-to-track cut efficiency from
  spallation products (sec. V B, Fig. 10).
- P. Lipari, "The fluxes of sub-cutoff particles detected by AMS, the cosmic ray albedo and atmospheric neutrinos",
  Astropart. Phys. 16 (2002) 295, DOI 10.1016/S0927-6505(01)00134-7, arXiv:astro-ph/0101559v1. Sub-cutoff particles
  in orbit as atmospheric secondaries (abstract).
- MINOS Collaboration, "The magnetized steel and scintillator calorimeters of the MINOS experiment", Nucl. Instrum.
  Methods A 596 (2008) 190-228, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2008.08.003, arXiv:0805.3170v2. Range versus curvature momentum
  of stopping muons (sec. 2.4.1); stopping cosmic muons as the MIP reference (sec. 3.8.3).
- MINOS Collaboration, "Measurement of the atmospheric muon charge ratio at TeV energies with MINOS", Phys. Rev. D
  76 (2007) 052003, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.76.052003, arXiv:0705.3815v4. Charge-sign significance cut (sec. III B 3);
  field-reversal ratios r_a, r_b and their geometric mean, equal-live-time pairs (sec. IV A, eqs. 2-6); (r_a - r_b)/2
  as the systematic of the combination (sec. IV D).
- A. A. Moiseev et al., "The anti-coincidence detector for the GLAST Large Area Telescope", Astropart. Phys. 27
  (2007) 339, DOI 10.1016/j.astropartphys.2006.12.003, arXiv:astro-ph/0702581v1. Segmented anticoincidence against
  self-veto (abstract); tile efficiency with cosmic muons (sec. 3.4).
- M. Motoki et al., "Precise measurements of atmospheric muon fluxes with the BESS spectrometer", Astropart. Phys.
  19 (2003) 113, DOI 10.1016/S0927-6505(02)00195-0, arXiv:astro-ph/0205344v1. Muon selection by a 1/beta band
  versus rigidity (sec. 4).
- Pierre Auger Collaboration, "Muon counting using silicon photomultipliers in the AMIGA detector of the Pierre Auger
  observatory", J. Instrum. 12 (2017) P03002, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/12/03/P03002, arXiv:1703.06193v2. Buried
  counters segmented in space and time against undercounting (sec. 3).
