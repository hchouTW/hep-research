# References: radiation damage and ageing

A split file of the annotated [bibliography](detector-principles-bibliography.md), in the same citation format: the sources of `radiation-and-aging-effects.md`. Entries that name an experiment or apparatus sit inside example blocks.

## Radiation damage and ageing

- E. Del Monte et al., "The effect of the displacement damage on the Charge Collection Efficiency in Silicon Drift
  Detectors for the LOFT satellite", JINST 10 (2015) P05002, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/10/05/P05002, arXiv:1503.07682v1
  (read 2026-11-19). Displacement damage from trapped protons in low Earth orbit; AP8 fluence via SPENVIS behind
  equivalent shielding (abstract; sec. 1, p. 3).
- E. Garutti and Yu. Musienko, "Radiation damage of SiPMs", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 926 (2019) 69,
  DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2018.10.191, arXiv:1809.06361v2 (read 2026-11-19/21). Dark-count rate as the dominant effect,
  occupancy loss of photon detection efficiency, annealing; lifetime fluence scale by environment (abstract; secs. 1,
  3.4, 6; eq. (10)).
- D. Miehling et al., "Lifetime and performance of the very latest microchannel-plate photomultipliers", Nucl.
  Instrum. Methods A 1049 (2023) 168047, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2023.168047, arXiv:2311.16676v1 (read 2026-11-19). QE
  loss with integrated anode charge from ion feedback, ALD coatings, lifetime test with a partly covered cathode
  (secs. 1 and 2.1).
- R. Padilla et al., "Effect of deep gain layer and Carbon infusion on LGAD radiation hardness", JINST 15 (2020)
  P10003, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/15/10/P10003, arXiv:2004.05260v4 (read 2026-11-19). Gain-layer acceptor removal,
  bias recovery, deep and carbonated gain layers (abstract; secs. 1-2).
- J. Řípa et al., "Characterization of more than three years of in-orbit radiation damage of SiPMs on GRBAlpha and
  VZLUSAT-2 CubeSats", Nucl. Instrum. Methods A 1076 (2025) 170513, DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2025.170513,
  arXiv:2411.00607v2 (3 Apr 2025; read 2026-11-19/21). In-orbit SiPM dark-rate and threshold growth; TID and TNID from AP-8/AE-8
  with Geant4-based tools (secs. 2, 3.2, 4.1, 6).
- SPENVIS (ESA Space Environment Information System), "Background: Trapped particle radiation models", help page,
  https://www.spenvis.oma.be/help/background/traprad/traprad.html, last update 27 Apr 2018 (read 2026-11-21). AP-8
  and AE-8 flux uncertainty: the widely quoted factor of 2, larger for differential fluxes made from the integral
  maps (sec. 5.1).
- M. Titov et al., "Summary and Outlook of the International Workshop on Aging Phenomena in Gaseous Detectors
  (DESY, Hamburg, October, 2001)", IEEE Trans. Nucl. Sci. 49 (2002) 1609, DOI 10.1109/TNS.2002.801666; also ICFA
  Instrum. Bull. 24 (2002) 22; arXiv:hep-ex/0204005v1 (read 2026-11-19/21). Ageing-rate definition and its limits,
  Malter effect, dependence on rate and particle type, in-situ collider chamber ageing and tests of collider
  chambers (secs. I, II.A, IV.A, V.D).

- K. Abe et al. (T2K Collaboration), "Scintillator ageing of the T2K near detectors from 2010 to 2021", JINST 17
  (2022) P10028, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/17/10/P10028, arXiv:2207.12982v1 (read 2026-11-19). Long-term ageing
  of unknown cause, with candidate factors (crazing, fogging, oxidation), scintillator versus WLS-fibre loss,
  extrapolation (abstract; secs. 1.2, 7).
- M. Aaboud et al. (ATLAS Collaboration), "Modelling radiation damage to pixel sensors in the ATLAS detector", JINST
  14 (2019) P06012, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/14/06/P06012, arXiv:1905.03739v2 (read 2026-11-19/21). Radiation damage in
  the digitization step; fluence per integrated luminosity refitted to leakage current through an annealing model;
  digitizer field, trapping and induced charge, with no annealing in the digitizer; annealing variation lies within
  the field-model and trapping-constant uncertainties; fluence range, full-depletion current and hardness-factor
  caveat of the leakage-current model (abstract; secs. 2, 3.1, 4, 4.2.3, 4.2.4, 4.5; eqs. (1)-(2); notes 5-6;
  Fig. 3; Table 5).
- ATLAS Collaboration, "Operation and performance of the ATLAS Tile Calorimeter in Run 1", Eur. Phys. J. C 78
  (2018) 987, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-018-6374-z, arXiv:1806.02129v2 (read 2026-11-19). Separating scintillator
  damage from PMT drift with laser, source and minimum-bias responses (sec. 4.5).
- CMS Collaboration, "Energy calibration and resolution of the CMS electromagnetic calorimeter in pp collisions at
  sqrt(s) = 7 TeV", JINST 8 (2013) P09009, DOI 10.1088/1748-0221/8/09/P09009, arXiv:1306.2016v2 (read 2026-11-19).
  Dose and fluence expectations, colour centres and self-annealing, light-monitoring correction and its in-situ
  tuning (secs. 2, 3, 4.1, 4.1.1; eq. (3)).
- M. Ackermann et al. (Fermi-LAT Collaboration), "The Fermi Large Area Telescope On Orbit: Event Classification,
  Instrument Response Functions, and Calibration", Astrophys. J. Suppl. 203 (2012) 4, DOI
  10.1088/0067-0049/203/1/4, arXiv:1206.1896v2 (read 2026-11-19/21). In-orbit crystal light-yield loss, SAA fraction,
  energy-scale drift fit over the first three years (secs. 2.1.2, 2.2, 7.3.2; eq. (53); Fig. 73).
