# Working rules: synthetic collider (illustrative)

- Everything this profile produces carries status `synthetic` and the word SYNTHETIC in titles, file names or labels.
- The generator's shape and cross section are inputs chosen for the example. Never present them, or anything measured from them, as a physics prediction or as a property of a real collider.
- The generator does not import theory code. A theory prediction is compared with this profile's measurement only through a `comparison-spec` (Path D), never by sharing code.
- Detector effects (efficiency, smearing, acceptance) are invented. Do not quote them as typical of any real detector.
- The empty evidence ledger is intentional: there is no source to cite. Any later real-data use needs a new profile with its own sources.
