# ePIC early running (proposed)

> Scope: ePIC experiment run plan proposed by the collaboration for early running; proposals, not EIC facility values.
> Method owner: [hep-measurement](../../../../../skills/hep-measurement/SKILL.md) for how run conditions enter a measurement design; this module adds only what the ePIC Early Science Report proposes.

Every number here is a proposed run plan (not a design target) from the ePIC Early Science Report, a collaboration report that calls it provisional. Facility design values are in [beam-configurations.md](../facility/beam-configurations.md); never present these proposals as facility values.

- Proposed configurations for the first five years: electron beam 9 GeV; e+Ag 9x115, e+D 9x130, e+p 9x130, e+p 9x275, e+Au 9x100 and e+³He 9x166 GeV, ion energies and e+A luminosities per nucleon, with integrated luminosities of 1.0, 1.5, 1.0, 2.5, 1.0 and 1.5 fb^-1 (eic:C82).
- Polarization: none in the first year; then longitudinally polarized electrons with transversely and later longitudinally polarized protons, expected to reach 70% for both beams (eic:C83).
- Stated limits: the 9 GeV electron beam limits the e+p center-of-mass energy to 100 GeV, compared with 140 GeV at 18x275 GeV, and per-species integrated luminosities of 1.0 to 2.5 fb^-1 fall short of the 10 fb^-1 or more per year expected for full operations (eic:C84).

A study that uses one of these configurations labels it a proposed run plan and states its integrated luminosity as the report's assumption.
