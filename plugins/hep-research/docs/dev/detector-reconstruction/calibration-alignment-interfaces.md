# Calibration and alignment interfaces (handoff, R37)

Status: written in T06 (P1). This file is the handoff to the later calibration and alignment plan (the C&A plan). It lists every interface reserved by R32-R36, with its status, its file locations and its fixtures. Paths are relative to `plugins/hep-research/`.

This plan derives no constants, performs no alignment and claims no calibration or alignment quality (R31). A passing check here shows that an interface behaves as stated on synthetic fixtures, nothing more. Payload sidecars hold a digest of their native content; they hold no constants.

Status words: **tested** means the interface has checks that pass on synthetic fixtures. **Designed** means the interface is specified and has no check yet; its owning task tests it. **Gap** means a backend cannot provide it, on a recorded probe (R37); a gap is never marked not-applicable.

## The alignment payload of the refit (D1 stage 9)

**Format, `t07-alignment-payload/1`.** One record per line, each ending in a newline, numbers at 17 significant digits; `fixture_tools/t07_alignment_payload.py` and `native/alignment_payload.cpp` read the same bytes and refuse the same defects.

```
t07-alignment-payload/1
parametrization t07-plane-rigid/1 local sensor
derived_against <64 lowercase hex: the nominal geometry identity>
origin nominal|measured
derived_from none|<64 lowercase hex: the content identity of its parent>
plane <1..8> <shift-loc0 mm> <shift-loc1 mm> <shift-normal mm> <rotation-loc0 rad> <rotation-loc1 rad> <rotation-normal rad>
end <number of plane records>
```

- **Mapping to plane deltas.** A plane record is that plane's `PlaneDelta` (`native/payload_geometry.hpp`) in the order of `t07-plane-rigid/1`, the R32 parametrization of the R33 alignment derivatives: moved = nominal · shift · R(normal) · R(loc1) · R(loc0), in the plane's local frame, level sensor. A plane without a record is at nominal. Planes appear in increasing order, each at most once.
- **Flag.** `acts_tracking_driver refit ... --alignment-payload FILE` applies it through the placement route, `PlacedTelescope::with_deltas` (user's choice 16). The payload is read and checked before any other input. Without the flag the refit is as before, byte for byte. `acts_tracking_driver geometry-id` prints the nominal geometry identity.
- **Refused, never applied (R32), exit 2.** A malformed file (another first line or parametrization, a record missing or out of order, a value that is not a finite decimal number, a plane record with another count of values, a wrong end count, text after the end, an unfinished last line), a plane the telescope does not have (outside 1 to 8), a plane twice, a payload labelled `nominal` that moves a plane (R32: nominal is the ideal geometry; fix round 1), and a payload derived against another nominal geometry. Every refusal names the alignment payload on stderr.
- **Identity rule.** The content identity of a payload is the SHA-256 of its bytes. A product that applied one records it in the header line `alignment_payload <content identity> <origin> <derived_against> <derived_from> <round>` of `acts-tracking-product/2`, right after `alignment_parametrization`, and only then, so a product with no payload is unchanged; `round` is `none`, since this format comes from no loop (R36). The R32 sidecar (`conditions-payload/1`, `payload_sidecar`) holds the content identity as `content_sha256` and no constants; its `payload_id` is `contracts.conditions.payload_identity`. Both product readers refuse the line unless its identities are 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters (`derived_from` may be `none`) and its round is `none` or a count. Readers before D1 stage 9 refuse a product carrying the line, so an old reader fails closed instead of ignoring a payload.
- **Derived payload (R33).** `derive_subset(parent, {plane: parameters})` keeps the chosen parameters, sets every other one to nominal, and writes `derived_from` as the parent's content identity. Its sidecar's `derived_from` is the parent sidecar's `payload_id`. Both differ from the parent's identities.
- **Nominal geometry identity.** The SHA-256 of `t07-telescope-nominal/1`: the eight planes of the frozen telescope as the driver builds them, each with its bounds and its local-to-global transform. It is not g4sim's `geometry_id` (D2), because the T07 telescope has no geometry IR from which one could be formed; this needs the coordinator's ruling (D1 stage 9 report); it departs from decisions.md's fixed identity (D2) and is a user item. It covers the plane bounds and local-to-global transforms only, not the world volume, the layer envelopes or the field. The deltas apply to `PlacedTelescope`'s copy of the nominal transforms, which are read from the same `build_telescope_geometry()`; that the two agree is asserted in the job (the zero-payload refit equals the plain geometry's `/1` records).
- **Origin.** The CAI-T07-3 payload holds the generated shift; it is labelled `measured` because R32 allows only nominal or measured and it is not the ideal geometry, with producer "external", source "synthetic CAI-T07-3 fixture: the generated shift, no measurement". It is no measurement (R31).

## Boundaries for the C&A plan

- Payload kinds are calibration constants, channel masks and alignment parameters. C0, C1 and C2 outputs enter through these kinds; a further kind is a new schema version.
- Conditions tags stay exactly as their source gives them (N11). Invalidation compares identities, never tags.
- Methods, weak-mode validation and loop rules stay in `skills/hep-detector/references/calibration-and-alignment.md` (R01).
- No conditions database or service is built (Section 3.1).
