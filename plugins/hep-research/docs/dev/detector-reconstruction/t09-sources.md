# T09: original method sources of BP06, by variant

The BP06 panel asks P0 to check the original method sources by variant (`t07-t09-task-sheets.md`, T09, open items; `t09-closeout.md`, BP06 sources row). The user approved a read-only literature check on 2026-11-11 (decisions.md, U-W6). Checked on 2026-11-11 through hep-citations: identifiers from the INSPIRE-HEP literature API, versions from the arXiv API, text from the arXiv e-print source of each paper. No source is cited from memory; each was found and read for this check.

Read levels are the contract's: `page` means the named sections were read (abstract and the method section), not the whole paper; `abstract+metadata` means the abstract only. Figures were not viewed. The sources are not imported into a literature record; this file is the record of the check. The searches were targeted lookups of the methods the reference and the recipe describe, not a systematic review, so a method stated in a source not read here is not excluded.

"The recipe" is `reco.light.channel-likelihood/1` as the close-out and `adapters/photon-likelihood` describe it. "The reference" is `skills/hep-detector/references/photon-likelihood-reconstruction.md`. Neither is changed by this check.

## Variant 1: all-channel likelihood with unhit channels and a shared threshold model

| Statement used | Source and what it states | Claim it bears on | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The likelihood runs over all channels, hit and unhit | t09:S01, eq. "hit and no-hit likelihood": `L_hit = prod_{i<=N_ch} P^hit,i prod_{i>N_ch}^{N_OM} P^no-hit,i`, "where N_ch is the number of hit OMs and N_OM the number of operational OMs". t09:S02, likelihood eq.: `L = prod_unhit P_j(unhit|mu_j) prod_hit {1 - P_i(unhit|mu_i)} f_q(q_i|mu_i) f_t(t_i|...)`. t09:S03, one-track likelihood: `prod_unhit (1 - P(i hit; x)) prod_hit P(i hit; x) f_q f_t`, "the products are taken over all unhit and hit PMTs" | Reference: "The likelihood multiplies over all channels"; recipe: "every live channel enters" | verified |
| Only live channels count; dead ones are excluded | t09:S01: the no-hit product runs to N_OM, "the number of operational OMs". No source read states the dead-versus-unhit rule in other words | Reference: "Only live, enabled channels count as unhit"; recipe's dead-channel gate | verified (by inference, t09:S01 only) |
| Hit and no-hit share one threshold model and sum to one per channel | t09:S01, amplitude likelihood: a hit term `P^hit (1 - P_th) P(A_i, mu)/...` and the zero-amplitude term `P_i(0) = P^no-hit + P^hit P_th`, with `P^hit = 1 - P^no-hit` and `P_th` "the probability that a signal of mu does not produce a pulse amplitude above the discriminator threshold". t09:S02: hit term `1 - P(unhit|mu)`, unhit term `P(unhit|mu)`, both "properties of the PMT and the electronics". t09:S03: `P(i hit; mu)` and `1 - P`, the same function, "established once using laser calibration data" from PMT occupancy | Reference: "P(no hit) is the probability of zero photoelectrons plus the probability that the signal stayed below threshold"; "the hit and unhit terms use the same threshold model, so ... sum to one"; recipe's per-channel sum gate | verified |
| Dark noise enters the no-hit probability | t09:S01: `P^no-hit -> P^no-hit P^no-noise`, `P^hit = 1 - P~^no-hit`; amplitude likelihood `P^no-hit = exp(-mu)(1 - P^noise)`. t09:S04: noise added to the Poisson mean, `lambda = E Lambda + rho`. t09:S05: noise rate `n` added to the photon intensity of every DOM | Reference: charge term "Poisson with mean mu_i plus the dark-noise mean"; recipe's dark mean in the charge model | verified |

## Variant 2: first-arrival time density with noise

| Statement used | Source and what it states | Claim it bears on | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| With single-time readout the recorded time is the first photoelectron's | t09:S01: "only the arrival time of the first pulse is recorded". t09:S03: "only the earliest photoelectron's time is reported when multiple photoelectrons are present in a hit". t09:S02: "a hit is recorded by the first photon arriving at a PMT, which leads a narrower distribution of hit time for higher numbers of incident photons" | Reference, "Several photons on one channel" | verified |
| `f_1(t) = mu f(t) exp(-mu F(t)) / (1 - exp(-mu))`, exact under Poisson | t09:S01, eq. "PSA": the first-of-N density `N p_1 (1 - P_1)^(N-1)` (MPE) convolved with the Poisson probability gives `p^1_mu = mu/(1 - e^-mu) p_1(t) e^(-mu P_1(t))`, the "Poisson Saturated Amplitude (PSA) p.d.f.", renormalized by `1 - e^-mu`. The source attributes PSA to two earlier works (its refs. BOUCHTA:1998:A, PORRATA:1997:A), not read here | Reference's formula; recipe's arrival density summed over charge (`arrival_response.py` docstring) | verified (as stated in t09:S01; the earlier originals not read) |
| The noise rate is included: `mu` is signal plus noise and `f`, `F` the normalized mixture over the window | t09:S01: noise hits "are included in the likelihood function by simply adding a constant p.d.f. p_0" after hit cleaning, not through `mu` and a mixture in the PSA density. t09:S05: in an extended (pulse-series) likelihood the noise rate is added to the intensity, `P(t_i) = lambda(t_i) + n`, over all DOMs; this is the same superposition, but for the full pulse series, not the first arrival. t09:S04: noise added to the Poisson mean of the counts. No source read writes the first-arrival density with noise inside `mu` and the mixture | Reference: "the noise rate is included: mu is the total mean ... (signal plus noise), and f and F are the corresponding normalized mixture"; recipe: Poisson process of intensity `mu_s phi + rho`, first arrival of that process | partly: each ingredient is sourced (PSA form; noise as an additive Poisson rate); their combination is the reference's and the recipe's own. t09:S01 treats noise differently (additive constant pdf); that is a cruder alternative, not a contradiction, since the reference's form follows from the superposition of Poisson processes |
| Using the single-photon density for bright channels biases the fit | t09:S01: "This first photon is usually less scattered than the average single photon, which modifies the probability distribution of the detected hit time"; t09:S02 as above. | Charge and time of one channel modelled jointly or conditionally | t09:S02: the time likelihood depends on the predicted charge `mu_i` (conditioning on the expectation, not on the observed charge). t09:S03: the time pdf mixes prompt and late components weighted by `P(prompt PE present | hit)` from Poisson means. ## Contradictions

None found. No source read contradicts the recipe or the reference. One tension is recorded (variant 3: t09:S01 compares track and cascade fits by raw likelihood, a practice BP06 excludes for T09). One difference of method is recorded (variant 2: t09:S01 adds noise as a constant pdf, where the reference and the recipe put it inside the Poisson intensity).

## Statements left without a source

- The first-arrival density with the noise rate inside `mu` and the mixture density (variant 2); derivable from the superposition of Poisson processes, but not stated in a source read.
- Saturated channels as a censored term (variant 1).
- Unhit channels left out as a cause of boundary bias (variant 1).
- The requirement that the time model describe the cleaned hits (variant 2).
- The penalty value 30 and its derivation (variant 3): the project's own (v4 section 7).
- Conditioning a channel's time on its observed charge (variant 2): the recipe's own; the sources condition on the expected charge.
