# T09 close-out against its sheet (D3 stage 5)

T09 is the photon likelihood (BP06), recipe `reco.light.channel-likelihood/1`, a development binding in Python (`adapters/photon-likelihood`).
Its sheet is in `t07-t09-task-sheets.md`. Paths are relative to `plugins/hep-research/`. Job evidence is in `<private>/evidence/t09-stage<N>-<job>/`,
and the job IDs are the ones `decisions.md` and the ledger record.

**Scope of this file.** One row per item of the sheet, with its status and evidence. Anything not met says why, and what would close it.
The sheet names no scenario for T09 (Section 16.1 has no A-ID for it), so BP06's panel is its acceptance (`t05-task-sheets.md`); the
panel's items that the sheet's bullets do not already name have rows of their own, under "Open item".

**Not judged here.** The quality verdict (pass or fail against `t04-values-v4.md` and `t04-prereg.md`) waits for K09 at Freeze B (finish-plan
D4). Nothing here pre-judges it. Every pull, failure, wrong-minimum, mis-ID, mirrored and reversed number these jobs printed on block 1 is a
development reading, not a gate. Every result is a shared-model closure (plan 11.1): the fixture and the fit share the analytic light model of
`t04-values-v4.md` 4.3, so it tests the code, not the model. No photon-likelihood quality or performance claim is made (R31).

**Status words** (as `t07-closeout.md` and `t08-closeout.md`).
- `met`: the evidence shows the item as the sheet states it.
- `met with readings`: met, with a limit or a departure stated beside it.
- `open`: work, or a decision, is still owed.
- `gap`: the backend cannot provide it, on a recorded probe (R37). Here the one gap is of another kind: no native backend is sought, by
  the user's recorded decision (U12 A), which the plan records as a gap in required delivery (K02).

**Evidence jobs.** The T09 stage 4 chain: main job 7575617 (hpa-wn33, PASS in 1 h 23 min 53 s), H2 array 7576269 (13 tasks, PASS) and
collection 7576270 (PASS). The main job reran every step of job 7571207 (stage 3), itself every step of job 7566770 (stage 2), and job 7566770
every step of job 7565328 (stage 1); its equality step: "equality lines matched: 216 (216 expected)" (`7575617.out`), the products, truth
files and READING and GATE lines of jobs 7566770 and 7571207 unchanged. Unless a row names another job, its evidence is job 7575617's step
log `<step>.log`, the array's `h2/<stratum>/task.log` or the collection's `collect/collect.log`. Rows that depend on the D3 stage 5 job say so;
it is job 7578442 (hpa-wn34, 10 min 02 s, `JOB RESULT PASS`, every step exit 0). It runs the example twice live, the example's test, the records test and the unit modules,
and shows the stage 4 code unchanged against job 7575617's manifest (`0c4e5c9c`, 822 files).

**Adapter record (this stage).** Adapter 0.2.0, status `demonstrated-on-synthetic-data` for its one tool and for the adapter; job of record
for adapter 0.2.0: job 7578442, the D3 stage 5 job (VALIDATION PHOTON-LIKELIHOOD-T09), with the stage 4 chain (jobs 7575617, 7576269, 7576270) the latest run
