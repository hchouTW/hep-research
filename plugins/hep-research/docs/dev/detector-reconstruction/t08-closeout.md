# T08 close-out against its sheet (D2 stage 5)

T08 is calorimetry (BP05), recipe `reco.calo.cell-cluster/1`, a development binding in Python (`adapters/calo-cluster`). Its sheet is in
`t07-t09-task-sheets.md`. Paths are relative to `plugins/hep-research/`. Job evidence is in `<private>/evidence/t08-stage<N>-<job>/`, and the
job IDs are the ones `decisions.md` and the ledger record.

**Scope of this file.** One row per item of the sheet, with its status and evidence. Anything not met says why, and what would close it.
The scenarios A15, A32 and A38 are in `t08-scenarios.md`; their rows here repeat its statuses.

**Not judged here.** The quality verdict (pass or fail against `t04-values-v4.md` and `t04-prereg.md`) waits for K09 at Freeze B (finish-plan
D4). Nothing here pre-judges it. Every pull, efficiency, merge and split number these jobs printed on block 1 is a development reading, not a
gate. No calorimetry quality or performance claim is made (R31).

**Status words** (as `t07-closeout.md`).
- `met`: the evidence shows the item as the sheet states it.
- `met with readings`: met, with a limit or a departure stated beside it.
- `open`: work, or a decision, is still owed.
- `gap`: the backend cannot provide it, on a recorded probe (R37). Here the one gap is of another kind: no native backend is sought, by
  the user's recorded decision (U12 A), which the plan records as a gap in required delivery (K02).

**Evidence job.** Job 7557963 (T08 stage 4, hpa-wn34, PASS in 14 min 03 s) reran every step of the earlier stages: the frozen-file and
generator checks, the five unit modules, the 15 development strata of t08-grid/1 block 1, CAI-T08-1 and -2, the zdcsim build, run and
reconstruction, and the stage 4 steps. Its equality steps show the earlier jobs' outputs unchanged: `stage1-equal.log` "PASS all 127
comparisons equal job 7552825's outputs", `stage2-equal.log` "PASS all 223 comparisons equal job 7554028's outputs", `stage3-equal.log` "PASS
all 233 comparisons equal job 7555555's outputs". Unless a row names another job, its evidence is that job's step log, `<step>.log`.

**Adapter record (this stage).** Adapter 0.2.0, status `demonstrated-on-synthetic-data` for both of its tools and for the adapter; job of
record for adapter 0.2.0: job 7557963 (VALIDATION CALO-CLUSTER-T08). That job ran the adapter's code and tests as 0.2.0 ships them; its
`adapter.json` then declared 0.1.0, `proposed`. Version 0.2.0 changes records only, so no new job ran in this stage.
