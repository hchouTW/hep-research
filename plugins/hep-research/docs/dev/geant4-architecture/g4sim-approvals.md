## How to approve

- Each item is approved by the person named, explicitly. Silence or a related remark is not approval (Section E7, item 1).
- For each approved item, the record holds the person, the date, and their words or a link to where they gave them.
- One explicit statement may cover several items if it names them, for example "I approve M1-M12, Q1-Q5 and D1-D20 as written". Its words are then recorded on each row. A change requested to a decision goes through Section E7, item 6, because Part B is frozen.
