## Notes on gate 7

- A corrupted field hash does not raise an error in `design.py`. Strict validation of the record fails, the study is unqualified, and the verdict falls to row 8 (`fidelity.insufficient`); `check` exits 0. The evaluation no longer counts as F4. This record takes that as the rejection the gate asks for; the derivation and kind corruptions are refused as errors.
- The faithful verdict is row 11 (`bound.direction`, inconclusive): the figure of merit is tail-sensitive and the study simulates no tail source, so the bound is optimistic.
- The control rule was changed after a first look. Under `shift-window/1` a control's shift fell outside 1.1 DL at 10 GV; the study then moved to `declared-size/1`, the rule the protocol names for new studies, with the injections from `plan-controls`. The study also moved to 1 GV, because a study at 10 GV decides no requirement.
- The scattering sample is 8,000 events, J15's. At 320 events the core width read 22% high, from blocks of 20 events.
- The study's two variations are in its table, and model confirmation for protons was not part of this gate.

## Defects found by these gates

- A field given by a parameter reached the IR as its name, and `g4sim_run` read it as 0 T without an error (`strtod`). The defect dates from S3; only kit specifications use a parameter there. `core/geometry` now evaluates it, and a number keeps its text, so no existing `geometry_id` changes. The C++ reader is unchanged.
- `design.py`'s comparison of `geometry_id` with the study could not fail: the study record carried no identity. It carries both now, and a missing identity is an error.
