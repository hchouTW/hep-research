## Notes on gate 7

- On the g4sim path `design.py` recomputes the declared field, realization and geometry hashes from the files the study names, and a record that says `validated-in-scope` while a condition is missing, out of scope or failed gets `design.qualification_mismatch`; `evaluate_design.py f4` keeps no such evaluation. This stops an edited record. It does not stop a forger who also rewrites the study's files and every hash that binds them: no check inside the project can.
- The error has no version gate: a 3.22 record with contradicted conditions moves from row 8 to an error. No recorded study is affected (415 run records scanned by the independent read; 10 validated, none fires). A study with no declared kind stays at row 8, as ART-31 and T46 require, but a hand label that declares a kind and has no benchmarks is an error.
- The faithful verdict is row 11 (`bound.direction`, inconclusive): the figure of merit is tail-sensitive and the study simulates no tail source, so the bound is optimistic.
- The control rule was changed after a first look. Under `shift-window/1` a control's shift fell outside 1.1 DL at 10 GV; the study then moved to `declared-size/1`, the rule the protocol names for new studies, with the injections from `plan-controls`. The study also moved to 1 GV, because a study at 10 GV decides no requirement.
- The scattering sample is 8,000 events, J15's. At 320 events the core width read 22% high, from blocks of 20 events.
- The study's two variations are in its table, and model confirmation for protons was not part of this gate.

G5 is due from a reviewer who is not the author, and is outstanding.

## Open items

- **G5** for the new C++, above.
- **Examples regenerated on E5**, a deviation from `docs/maintenance.md` on the maintainer's word: 77 files, 28 numbers changed at the last bit (2.7e-16 relative at most).
- **Encoded cellID limits.** The composition is a table by template and copy, not Section 6.2.6's copy number times stride, a deviation the maintainer accepted; so a template under two mothers with different indices is refused and must be declared as two templates. A field too narrow is found only when a run reaches the value, so a run that passes does not show the field is wide enough. Pre-S5 hit files are refused by the new `simroot`; read them with an earlier build. `edep_visible_ly_split` and `edep_by_dedx_class` are refused at `event_file`, so the part decoding in `CellId` is not exercised. `select` has no copy-level filter, so the cellID mask of Section 6.3 does not exist and is not in `request_id`. `simreco_strip` still takes one crossing per cell and event, on the volume cell. A channel stream still uses the volume cell.
- **Gaps accepted by the maintainer**: TOF time resolution, calorimeter transverse shape, hit efficiency, beam-test sampling fraction, tracker residuals against data, longitudinal shape as a weak band.
- Gate 6's scattering rows ran with a zero field attached, before the kit stopped attaching one. The physics is the same, and they were not rerun.
- `StripSigma` is corrected after S5, with J15 regenerated on E2; no S5 closure ran with `noise-digitization` on.
- `simulation_block` records the first field of a detector, and `element_view` one field over the world: reconstruction of a detector with several fields is not supported.
- Earlier limits stand (`g4sim-gates/S4.md`).
