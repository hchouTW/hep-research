## Notes

- An IR placement belongs to a logical, so an overlay entry moves that daughter in every touchable of its mother; the gate's module shifts move those modules in all 64 layer-4 ladders. A touchable of its own needs a logical of its own in the specification (`core/geometry/alignment.py`).
- S6's AMS-02 TOF case could not serve the gate: its counters fill their mothers, so any shift fails containment, as the check should.
- Conditions enter the response layer only (`core/simulation/digitize.py`): the gain factor multiplies the sensor gain, a threshold replaces the electronics threshold, a dead channel reads no code, every draw still made. `g4sim_digi` does not read conditions, and `digitize.job_with_keys` refuses a job that carries them.
