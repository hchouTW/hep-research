### Fixes after the second independent read; gate 5 judged on build 26 (2026-11-08)

On the maintainer's word ("do it based on your recommendation") the second independent read's findings were fixed (`62e69568`), and a read of those fixes found two more, also fixed (`4ee3288a`):

- XTR broke energy conservation: Geant4's XTR processes add each TR photon without taking its energy from the parent. In the XTR check, 1,567 of 2,000 events had residuals up to 0.14 MeV, and the run still reported ok. The TR photons are now a source term (`xtr_created_MeV`); residual 4.5e-13 MeV.
- optical_scope: the entry into an out-of-scope volume is judged by direction across the boundary, not by OpBoundary's status (`SameMaterial` let photons through). A photon born outside the scope is killed at zero length. The new column `optical_steps_outside` counts steps outside. The surface normal is asked for only at boundary steps (it had produced 26,008 GeomNav0003 warnings for 15,010 photons), and an invalid normal there is fatal. The program refuses to run if KillProcess's post-step action would come before OpBoundary's.
- The per-track end map is no longer kept for optical photons (its memory grew with the photon count). Time-cut kills have their own column, `optical_killed_time`.
- A full-turn G4Polycone resets its start to 0 (observed on 11.4.3): the read-back does not compare the start then.
