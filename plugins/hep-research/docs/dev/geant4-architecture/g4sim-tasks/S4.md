# g4sim phase S4: Hits and response

## Background

Phase S4 of the g4sim plan (Part B of `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task.md`, Section 19.2). Labels S0-S9, T1, T2 and GB1-GB24 are local to that plan (Section 1).

This card uses the headings `lint_task.py` requires, and carries the eight sections of Section E5, in E5's order, as level-4 headings under them: Goal, Scope checklist, Deliverables, To-verify list, Thresholds and test designs, Environments, Risks and stop points, Execution log.

Pre-registration (Section 19.2): this card is fixed by its SHA-256 in `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task-registry.json` before any run a threshold judges. Thresholds may be tightened, never loosened. An entry that only appends to the Execution log is registered again with that reason, so the thresholds keep their earlier registration.

11. G5 confirmation of `Philox.hh` and hit output.

**Phase-specific values and rules**

- Gates 1-6 are exact (known-answer vectors and bit-identity).
- Gate 7: TOST, α = 0.05, mean ±0.5%, variance ±3%, r = 1 and r = 0.5; each test's power 0.9^(1/K), joint power ≥ 0.9. K and sample size are written here at refresh, before the test.
- Gate 7, from an independent trial of 12 replicas on a real `g4sim_run` output (1,000 e+ of 1 to 60 MeV in a scintillator block, LY 100/MeV, 1 mm bins, 36,523 bins: 4,300 Poisson, 32,223 Gaussian; `s4_gates/scintillation_equivalence.py`): K = 8, from r (1, 0.5) × regime (Poisson, Gaussian) × moment (mean, variance). Each test's power 0.9^(1/8) = 0.9869, z = 4.126. Needed: 61 replicas, from the Poisson mean at 262,003 draws. Registered: 80 replicas, numbers 2000 to 2079, a factor 1.3 for the trial's error; the trial's replicas 1000 to 1011 are not reused. Statistics d1 = N - μ and d2 = (N - μ)² - v over draws, margins relative to the mean of μ and of v.
- Section 10.2: an error is raised when the output-group index reaches 2¹⁶. The upper bound on the probability of this error is written here once `response-pipeline/1` fixes draws per sub-stream.
- Limits: first_event + number of events ≤ 2³²; sub-index < 2¹⁶.
- Rule: if E2 and E5 sampled values disagree (Section 22), cross-platform tolerances and boundary rules are registered before the gates that use them. The throughput budget is written in this phase.

- Compile flags and keys, Section 22: `Philox.hh` refuses `-ffast-math` at compile time, and its fsum keeps exact IEEE operations with `volatile` on the rounding steps; `geant4-config --cflags` here has `-march=nocona` and no fast-math. Bit-identical with `rng.py` on 2,002 Philox cases and with `math.fsum` on 304 sets, in `tests/adapters/test_g4sim_philox.py`.
- Section 22 designs, in `core/simulation/response.py` and `response-pipeline/1`. Photon times: a detected photon takes one output group, for its emission delay and transit time; a bin can exceed 2^16 groups, so times go in blocks of 2^15 photons, each with its own stage name `<stage>/time/<block>` and so its own key. Gamma factor: stage `yield_variation`, a volume stream with the volume ID and sub-index 0, one draw per event and volume, each attempt two output groups. Bound on exhausting a sub-stream, from a Chernoff bound with acceptance at least 1/2 and two groups per attempt: below 1e-300 at 1,024 draws per sub-stream, the design limit on channels. The acceptance floor of 1/2 is an assumption of the bound, not measured here. Charge sharing is a declared table, by the maintainer's option A.
