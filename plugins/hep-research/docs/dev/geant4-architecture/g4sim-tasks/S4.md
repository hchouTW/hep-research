# g4sim phase S4: Hits and response

## Background

Phase S4 of the g4sim plan (Part B of `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task.md`, Section 19.2). Labels S0-S9, T1, T2 and GB1-GB24 are local to that plan (Section 1).

This card uses the headings `lint_task.py` requires, and carries the eight sections of Section E5, in E5's order, as level-4 headings under them: Goal, Scope checklist, Deliverables, To-verify list, Thresholds and test designs, Environments, Risks and stop points, Execution log.

Pre-registration (Section 19.2): this card is fixed by its SHA-256 in `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task-registry.json` before any run a threshold judges. Thresholds may be tightened, never loosened. An entry that only appends to the Execution log is registered again with that reason, so the thresholds keep their earlier registration.

## Objective

#### Goal

Deliver hits and the response pipeline: `simulation-hits/1`, `response-pipeline/1`, `efficiency-map/1`, `external` sources, reference planes, truth keeping, Philox random numbers with channel and volume streams, sampling, correctly rounded summation, the scintillation formula and the FE4 interface (Section 19.2, S4).

**This phase's negative tests (Section 15.1)**

- [ ] Specification: first_event + number of events ≤ 2³²; Philox keys pairwise distinct within a study. Negative test: Exceeding the limit; two readout systems with the same name
- [ ] Response: The three levels of determinism; chunk merging passes partial sums. Negative test: Switching to a stateful generator; switching to sequential summation; switching to rounding each chunk before merging
- [ ] Response: Endpoints of uniform variates and word order. Negative test: Switching to a formula that can produce 1.0; swapping x₀ and x₁
- [ ] Response: fsum agrees with CPython; range of summands. Negative test: Removing the final correction step; including a summand of 1×10³⁰⁰
- [ ] Response: Volume streams shared between readout systems; photons distributed among channels by a multinomial. Negative test: Switching to channel-stream sampling; switching to independent Poisson per channel
- [ ] Response: Layout of counter word 3: each draw starts from a new output group; fixed order within a sub-stream; chunks do not enter the counter. Negative test: Letting two draws share an output group; writing the chunk index into word 3 (the output then changes when the number of chunks changes, and this must be caught); a segmentation with sub-index ≥ 2¹⁶ must be rejected by the response checks
- [ ] Output: Closure of `edep_visible_ly_split`: E^L + E^S equals `edep_visible`; n^L equals the number of steps with μ_s > 10; the material's scintillation yield and `RESOLUTIONSCALE`. Negative test: Putting a step with μ_s = 10 into E^L; requesting this quantity for a material without a scintillation yield
- [ ] Specification: `external` sources; reference planes. Negative test: An `external` source used by the calibration workflow must be rejected; a reference plane without a coordinate frame

#### Thresholds and test designs

**Gates, verbatim from Section 19.2**

1. Philox passes the known-answer vectors.
2. Tests of the endpoints of uniform variates and word order pass.
3. Output is bit-identical after shuffling order and changing the number of chunks.
4. The test vectors of fsum and partial-sum merging pass.
5. Two readout systems using the same cellID get different channel streams.
6. Two readout systems reading the same volume get the same photon-number draws.
7. The scintillation formula at r = 1 and r = 0.5 is equivalent in mean and variance to the analytic expressions of Section 10.3 (TOST, α = 0.05; mean ±0.5%, variance ±3%; all tests must pass, joint power ≥ 0.9 per Section 18, each test 0.9^(1/K), K written into the task card, sample size derived from this); the input E^L and E^S come from `g4sim_run`'s `edep_visible_ly_split`, not from a hand-written fixture.
8. Hit files read back through simroot agree with the scorer sums.
9. J15 results unchanged.
10. This phase's negative tests.
11. G5 confirmation of `Philox.hh` and hit output.

**Phase-specific values and rules**

- Gates 1-6 are exact (known-answer vectors and bit-identity).
- Gate 7: TOST, α = 0.05, mean ±0.5%, variance ±3%, r = 1 and r = 0.5; each test's power 0.9^(1/K), joint power ≥ 0.9. K and sample size are written here at refresh, before the test.
- Section 10.2: an error is raised when the output-group index reaches 2¹⁶. The upper bound on the probability of this error is written here once `response-pipeline/1` fixes draws per sub-stream.
- Limits: first_event + number of events ≤ 2³²; sub-index < 2¹⁶.
- Rule: if E2 and E5 sampled values disagree (Section 22), cross-platform tolerances and boundary rules are registered before the gates that use them. The throughput budget is written in this phase.

#### Execution log

- Cross-platform sampling, Section 22: 200,000 draws per sampler at `8b75203c`, E5 (Linux x86_64, glibc) against E2 (Darwin arm64), Python 3.13.2 on both. Identical: `uniform`, `poisson_inverse`, `poisson_ptrs`, `binomial_inverse`, `binomial_btpe`. Different: `gaussian`, `exponential`, `gamma`. So transcendental results are not bit-identical across libm, as the plan allowed for; the integer layer and every discrete sampler tested agree. A tolerance in ulps still has to be measured and declared.
- Section 22 designs, in `core/simulation/response.py` and `response-pipeline/1`. Photon times: a detected photon takes one output group, for its emission delay and transit time; a bin can exceed 2^16 groups, so times go in blocks of 2^15 photons, each with its own stage name `<stage>/time/<block>` and so its own key. Gamma factor: stage `yield_variation`, a volume stream with the volume ID and sub-index 0, one draw per event and volume, each attempt two output groups. Bound on exhausting a sub-stream, from a Chernoff bound with acceptance at least 1/2 and two groups per attempt: below 1e-300 at 1,024 draws per sub-stream, the design limit on channels. The acceptance floor of 1/2 is an assumption of the bound, not measured here. Charge sharing is a declared table, by the maintainer's option A.
- References, Section 22, verified 2026-11-07 at a primary source, metadata level (Crossref, query by title): Philox, Salmon, Moraes, Dror, Shaw, SC11 (2011) 1-12, doi:10.1145/2063384.2063405; PTRS, Hormann, Insurance: Mathematics and Economics 12 (1993) 39-45, doi:10.1016/0167-6687(93)90997-4; BTPE, Kachitvichyanukul, Schmeiser, CACM 31 (1988) 216-222, doi:10.1145/42372.42381; Gamma, Marsaglia, Tsang, ACM TOMS 26 (2000) 363-372, doi:10.1145/358407.358414. The papers' full text was not read. Known-answer vectors: Random123 `tests/kat_vectors`, read in full for `philox4x32 10`. The constants of PTRS and BTPE were read from NumPy's `distributions.c`, whose comment records that the 1988 paper's final test has two error terms with the wrong sign; `rng.py` uses the corrected form.
- 2026-11-07: S4 started on `g4sim/S4` from main `737fa3e6`, after the 0.58.0 release. It stopped at once on Section 22 and resumed on the maintainer's words, in `g4sim-approvals.md`.

Carried from S3 (addendum 11.1, 2026-11-07), to settle when S4 starts: Section 10.4 names only extra variance for two components, but truncation also shifts the means, by -45% and +105% at small yields in the GB15 trial; the r = 0.15 feasibility holds for that trial's smooth μ_s distribution; `G4OpticalPhysics` attaches `G4EmSaturation` itself, unlike what Section 10.3 says.
