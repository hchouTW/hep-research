# g4sim phase S2: g4sim_run and regression

## Background

Phase S2 of the g4sim plan (Part B of `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task.md`, Section 19.2). Labels S0-S9, T1, T2 and GB1-GB24 are local to that plan (Section 1).

This card uses the headings `lint_task.py` requires, and carries the eight sections of Section E5, in E5's order, as level-4 headings under them: Goal, Scope checklist, Deliverables, To-verify list, Thresholds and test designs, Environments, Risks and stop points, Execution log.

Pre-registration (Section 19.2): this card is fixed by its SHA-256 in `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task-registry.json` before any run a threshold judges. Thresholds may be tightened, never loosened. An entry that only appends to the Execution log is registered again with that reason, so the thresholds keep their earlier registration.

## Objective

#### Goal

Deliver `g4sim_run` with the old feature set: volume-tree write-back, the field builder, geometry-check mode, the SD dispatcher, the legacy observer and `legacy` accounting, termination classes and the unreleased-energy table, deterministic merging, check-spec and dataset control, and pass regression of the inventory table (Section 19.2, S2).

**Phase-specific values and rules**

- T1: bit-identical, comparison scope per the inventory table.
- T2 (Section 18, D12): no T2 run exists in S2, because all 112 rows of `g4sim-inventory.json` are class T1-recorded. No margin, K or T2 position tolerance is registered. If a run falls to T2, stop and register them under R2 before its first T2 run.
- Conservation limits of legacy physics lists, registered before any judged run (Section 8.9; design by hep-detector, values accepted by the maintainer on 2026-11-05, `g4sim-approvals.md`). E is the event's incident energy equivalent. They come from the unaccounted energy sources of each list and never from the run under test.
  - Pure EM (`G4EmStandardPhysics`, `_option3`, `_option4`, with or without decay physics): q_max = 1×10⁻⁷ E, r_max = 1×10⁻⁸ E. No physical term is unaccounted, so only rounding remains. These values are also GB8's per-event residual tolerance.
  - Hadronic (`FTFP_BERT_EMZ`, `FTFP_BERT_EMY`, `FTFP_BERT__SS`, `QGSP_BIC_EMZ`): q_max = 0.60 E + 1 GeV, r_max = 0.40 E. Basis: binding losses up to 40 % of the non-electromagnetic energy (Lee et al., NIM A 882 (2018) 148, doi:10.1016/j.nima.2017.10.087; PDG 2025 Section 35.10.3 gives 20-40 % of hadronic energy), taken with all energy non-electromagnetic; relative fluctuation 0.044/0.304 (50 GeV pions in copper, Lee Fig. 7a) to the 99.9 % level; 1 GeV is the violation Geant4 tolerates in one interaction before resampling (`G4HadronicInteraction.cc`, 11.4.3), and it also holds fission and capture releases.
  - Section 22's question on escaping neutrons: yes, each books 0.78 MeV never incident, a negative systematic. Each freed neutron costs at least about 4 MeV of binding (AME2020), so this stays below about 0.08 E and inside the limits.
  - Limits of this basis: the sourced values are for pions in copper and lead with Geant4 10.3, some read from figures. None is for protons, silicon, plastic or Geant4 11.4.3. Beams reach 1 TeV, as the sources do. Terms smaller than the hadronic limits, probably `killed_unbooked`, go under "accounting terms that cannot be detected" (Section 19.2).
- Absolute check (Section 8.9): calibration run N ≥ 2995 events; one-sided 95% upper bound of q̂ ≤ q_max; 95% interval of |r̄| within ±r_max; joint pass probability of a correct program ≥ 0.9.
- Relative check (Section 8.9): one-sided Fisher exact test, nominal false-positive rate 0.05, negative-control power ≥ 0.9 at that n and every larger n.
- Two-step registration: N, p_max, σ_r, μ_r, p₀, p₁ and any undetectable accounting term are computed from an independent trial run by the rules of Section 8.9 and registered before the test run. A trial run is never reused.
- Gate 6: bit-identical in MT with 1, 2 and 4 threads for `per-event` seeding.

**Prototype criteria, verbatim defaults from Section 23**

- GB3: One template placed under both a mother with a field and a mother without; built once in forward and once in reverse order, the written-back field managers both equal the IR; setting only the mother differs from the IR in at least one order (confirming that the propagation rule really exists); after a charged particle is transported by `G4Transportation` through both places, the radius of curvature fitted from the trajectory differs from p/(0.3 Z B) by ≤ 1×10⁻³ (relative), and the trajectory in the field-free place is straight (deviation ≤ 1×10⁻⁹ mm); in MT, every worker's write-back equals the master's; in a field-free volume, the step sequences with a nullptr manager and with no manager at all are bit-identical
- GB5: Steps counted by the SteppingAction by pre-step volume equal, event by event, the numbers of steps the SDs receive; with two child SDs on the same logical, both receive the same number of steps
- GB6: Confirmed by the T1 of the S2 gates itself; negative: registering one extra step-limit process in legacy mode changes the output of at least one event
- GB7: Every particle's process list in the effective config contains `G4UserSpecialCuts`; with a minimum kinetic energy set, the sum of the kinetic energies of tracks it kills equals the sum of those steps' deposits (relative difference ≤ 1×10⁻¹²); with the maximum step set to one tenth of the EM limit, every e± step length is ≤ the maximum step
- GB8: A run with hadronic physics, a magnetic field and a deliberately lowered looper threshold: every track has exactly one class (accounting-error count 0); loopers' killed kinetic energy > 0 and not in the deposit; per-event conservation residual of a pure-EM run ≤ the task card's tolerance
- GB9: The values for a fixed particle list (n, μ±, π±, K±, Λ) differ from reference values computed independently from PDG masses by ≤ 1×10⁻⁶ (relative); without radioactive decay in the physics list, the value for nuclei is 0
- GB18: After rebuilding materials from the re-acquisition record (density, mass fractions, state, temperature, pressure, mean excitation energy and elements), the material table and derived quantities (radiation length, nuclear interaction length, electron density) in the effective config equal the derived quantities recorded by the re-acquisition build's ProbeV2 (ProbeV2 records these derived quantities; the old SimProbe does not); T1 itself is the final confirmation

- Section 22, datasets: `contracts/rules/geant4_datasets.json`, from `datasets/probe_datasets.py` on E5, 10 protons per case. Hadronic lists: G4ENSDFSTATEDATA, G4LEDATA, G4PARTICLEXSDATA and G4LEVELGAMMADATA; EM lists: G4ENSDFSTATEDATA and G4LEDATA. Without G4LEVELGAMMADATA a hadronic run still ends with exit 0 and different events, so dataset control must refuse it. `FTFP_BERT__SS` timed out at 900 s even with nothing changed, so its G4LEVELGAMMADATA entry follows the FTFP_BERT family and was not observed. The first attempt, at 100 events, crashed on that timeout and saved nothing.
- `reacquire/recorded_runs.json` is the facts table of the 112 runs, in the repository so a baseline can be rerun from the repository and its records alone.
- `g4sim_run/`: one binary. The two old observers are ported line for line under the namespaces `legacy_element` and `legacy_layered`, so both fit in it; `legacy_block` carries `observer_revision`. Decision: the 5 runs built from `3f4f9985` use `layered-early`, because `layered_sim` at that commit had no `leak_rear_MeV`, `leak_front_MeV` and `leak_lateral_MeV` columns.
- Decision: `IrTree.hh` recovers the doubles of a non-NIST material from the record alone. The record holds density divided by its unit, which can differ by one ulp from the old program's value, and for a one-element material Geant4 uses its NIST element, so the recorded A is not the value passed in. The neighbour that reproduces every recorded quantity is taken; a material with none is refused (GB18).
- Section 22, D0 on the g4sim path: the mapping is in Section 19.2 under S5 (geantino material scan, field comparison by field-configuration hash, reco-closure, tracking reference); nothing is implemented in S2. Implementation and proof follow in S5, gate 7.
