# g4sim phase S6: First reference cases

## Background

Phase S6 of the g4sim plan (Part B of `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task.md`, Section 19.2). Labels S0-S9, T1, T2 and GB1-GB24 are local to that plan (Section 1).

This card uses the headings `lint_task.py` requires, and carries the eight sections of Section E5, in E5's order, as level-4 headings under them: Goal, Scope checklist, Deliverables, To-verify list, Thresholds and test designs, Environments, Risks and stop points, Execution log.

Pre-registration (Section 19.2): this card is fixed by its SHA-256 in `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task-registry.json` before any run a threshold judges. Thresholds may be tightened, never loosened. An entry that only appends to the Execution log is registered again with that reason, so the thresholds keep their earlier registration.

#### Thresholds and test designs

**Gates, verbatim from Section 19.2**

1. Each case's residuals are within tolerance; tolerances are written into the task card and fixed before S6 starts, `case.json` only copies the task card's values, and the gate does not pass if the two differ.
2. Every case records the result of verifying the independence of its reference values.
3. A case is marked `validated-in-scope` only when the conditions of Section 13.3 hold.
4. In `coverage.json`, every module used by the S6 cases is covered by at least one case.

**Cases, tolerances and negative controls, registered before any run** (2026-11-08; values verified with hep-citations on 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-08 and written into the ledgers first: `detector-atlas` for CMS, `ams-02` for AMS-02, `eic` for ePIC; decisions in `g4sim-approvals.md`, "Before S6"). Each quantity is measured by a geantino through the case's detector-spec, its path in each material weighted by the radiation length Geant4 reports for that material, or counted from the expanded IR. `case.json` copies these values; a difference fails gate 1.

| Case | Level | Quantity | Reference value, independent of Geant4 | Tolerance | Negative control, which must fail the same tolerance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CMS ECAL barrel crystal | L1 | x/X0 along the crystal axis, front-face centre to rear-face centre, PbWO4 at CMS's 8.28 g/cm3 | 25.77: 230 mm (CMS, JINST 2008 p. 92) x 8.28 g/cm3 / 7.39 g/cm2 (PDG, PbWO4); CMS states 25.8 | 1.0% relative | The crystal 220 mm long |
| AMS-02 TOF, one half | L1 | x/X0 of the scintillator at normal incidence through one counter of each layer of a half, outside overlaps, EJ-200 at 1.023 g/cm3 | 0.0466: 2 x 1 cm x 1.023 g/cm3 / 43.90 g/cm2 (PDG, polyvinyltoluene); NIM A 623 Table 3 states 4.7% | 3.0% relative | Counters 0.9 cm thick |
| AMS-02 TOF, counters | L1 | Number of counters in the expanded IR | 34 (8, 8, 10, 8; NIM A 623 p. 972) | exact | One counter fewer in layer 3 |
| ePIC ZDC hadronic section | L2 | x/X0 of the steel along the section's axis (64 absorber plates and the back plate, Steel235 at 7.85 g/cm3) | 73.73: 65 x 2 cm x 7.85 g/cm3 / 13.84 g/cm2 (PDG, iron) | 1.0% relative | 60 layers instead of 64 |
| ePIC ZDC hadronic section | L2 | Readout cells of the scintillator layers in the expanded IR | 8,096 (12 x 11 cells in even layers, 11 x 11 in odd, 64 layers; ePIC geometry 26.11.0; also stated in the 2026 Preins talk) | exact | Even layers with 11 x 11 cells (7,744) |

Detection limits: each case's detection limit is its tolerance; every negative control changes its quantity by more than that (CMS -4.3%, AMS -10% and one counter, ZDC -6.2% and 352 cells). Each case is labelled "an approximate reconstruction from the cited claims, not the experiment's geometry". Known approximations, declared in each `case.json`: the CMS crystal is one tapered barrel crystal, 22 x 22 to 26 x 26 mm2 nominal; the AMS-02 trapezoidal counters, whose outline no source dimensions, are rectangles of their largest width, and the planes are 120 cm apart ("about 120 cm"); the ZDC's staggered grid is modelled by even and odd layers of their own cell counts, and its EM section is left out.

**Phase-specific values and rules**

- Rule: each case's tolerance is written here before S6 starts, after the values are verified; a difference between this card and `case.json` fails gate 1.
- Rule: a case's negative control and detection limit are stated here for any case aiming at `validated-in-scope` (Section 13.3).
