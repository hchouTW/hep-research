# g4sim phase S6: First reference cases

## Background

Phase S6 of the g4sim plan (Part B of `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task.md`, Section 19.2). Labels S0-S9, T1, T2 and GB1-GB24 are local to that plan (Section 1).

This card uses the headings `lint_task.py` requires, and carries the eight sections of Section E5, in E5's order, as level-4 headings under them: Goal, Scope checklist, Deliverables, To-verify list, Thresholds and test designs, Environments, Risks and stop points, Execution log.

Pre-registration (Section 19.2): this card is fixed by its SHA-256 in `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task-registry.json` before any run a threshold judges. Thresholds may be tightened, never loosened. An entry that only appends to the Execution log is registered again with that reason, so the thresholds keep their earlier registration.

## Objective

#### Goal

Add `reference:geometry-cases` with three first cases, CMS ECAL (L1), ePIC ZDC (L2) and AMS-02 TOF (L1), each with cited values, a recorded independence result and tolerances fixed in advance (Section 19.2, S6).

#### Thresholds and test designs

**Gates, verbatim from Section 19.2**

1. Each case's residuals are within tolerance; tolerances are written into the task card and fixed before S6 starts, `case.json` only copies the task card's values, and the gate does not pass if the two differ.
2. Every case records the result of verifying the independence of its reference values.
3. A case is marked `validated-in-scope` only when the conditions of Section 13.3 hold.
4. In `coverage.json`, every module used by the S6 cases is covered by at least one case.

**Phase-specific values and rules**

- Rule: each case's tolerance is written here before S6 starts, after the values are verified; a difference between this card and `case.json` fails gate 1.
- Rule: a case's negative control and detection limit are stated here for any case aiming at `validated-in-scope` (Section 13.3).
