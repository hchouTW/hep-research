This card uses the headings `lint_task.py` requires, and carries the eight sections of Section E5, in E5's order, as level-4 headings under them: Goal, Scope checklist, Deliverables, To-verify list, Thresholds and test designs, Environments, Risks and stop points, Execution log.

#### To-verify list

#### Thresholds and test designs

**Gates, verbatim from Section 19.2**

1. Two IRs differing only by an alignment overlay have different `geometry_id`.
2. The placements listed by the diff tool are exactly those overlaid.
3. This phase's negative tests.

**Phase-specific values and rules**

- Gates 1, 2: exact (identity and diff listing). No tolerance applies. Registered design (`s9_gates/alignment_gate.py`): S7's CMS Phase-1 barrel pixel case and four overlays of its layer 4, each applied alone: (A) three modules shifted along their ladder by 1 µm and ladder 84 by (10, −10, 20) µm; (B) ladder 90 rotated by 0.001° about its centre; (C) a zero shift of one module; (D) A and B together. Gate 1: the nominal IR and the four overlaid IRs have five distinct `geometry_id`s, and every overlaid IR passes check-spec (gap rule and error bounds included). Gate 2: for each overlay, `g4sim_run.py diff` lists as moved exactly the placements shifted or turned by a non-zero amount, as overlaid exactly the placements the overlay names, and no placement held by one IR alone.
- Gate 3: `s9_gates/negatives.py`, the two rows of Section 15.1 for S9 as code changes (an overlay referencing a non-existent placement accepted; snapshots with overlapping IOVs accepted), each caught by its named test.
- No Geant4 run and no new C++: S9 has no G5. Conditions enter `core/simulation/digitize.py` only; `g4sim_digi` does not read them and its job builder refuses such a job.
