# g4sim phase S5: Kits and the design loop

## Background

Phase S5 of the g4sim plan (Part B of `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task.md`, Section 19.2). Labels S0-S9, T1, T2 and GB1-GB24 are local to that plan (Section 1).

This card uses the headings `lint_task.py` requires, and carries the eight sections of Section E5, in E5's order, as level-4 headings under them: Goal, Scope checklist, Deliverables, To-verify list, Thresholds and test designs, Environments, Risks and stop points, Execution log.

Pre-registration (Section 19.2): this card is fixed by its SHA-256 in `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task-registry.json` before any run a threshold judges. Thresholds may be tightened, never loosened. An entry that only appends to the Execution log is registered again with that reason, so the thresholds keep their earlier registration.

#### Thresholds and test designs

**Gates, verbatim from Section 19.2**

1. J15's evaluation is exactly the same with the modified `design.py` and `evaluate_design.py` (layered-spectrometer takes the retained current path).
2. Existing layered-spectrometer candidates give exactly the same design geometry file, `parameter_digest`, specification files and verdict with the modified `design.py` and `evaluate_design.py` (`geometry`, `f4`, and the `search` path via `from-geometry`).
3. When a derivation record is tampered with, `design.py` rejects (negative test).
4. When a candidate's `geometry_kind` does not match the kind of the design geometry file, `design.py` rejects (negative test).
5. Every benchmark using two physics lists has a model-confirmation record.
6. Every benchmark's negative control is detected.
7. End to end: a synthetic study generated by a kit and executed through `g4sim_run` obtains an F4 evaluation through `check_geant4_study.py` and `design.py`, with all four D0 conditions judged from the records; corrupting the input of one condition (for example a mismatched field hash) must make `design.py` reject.
8. Design search for new geometry kinds: a synthetic design space for an S5 kit (for example calorimeter) completes one search through `evaluate_design.py search` → `g4kit.py` → `geant4_run.py`, every candidate having a derivation record, specification-file hashes and a computational-run; changing one byte of a specification file written by `g4kit.py` must make `geant4_run.py` reject on hash mismatch.

**Phase-specific values and rules**

- Gates 1, 2: exact equality of evaluation, design geometry file, `parameter_digest`, specification files and verdict.
- Gates 3, 4, 7, 8: rejection on the stated tampering.
- Rule: each kit benchmark's tolerance and negative control (Section 15.3) are written here before its first run, with reference data verified by hep-citations. A benchmark with no usable reference is a gap, not a pass.

**Kit benchmarks, registered before any run** (the maintainer's decision, `g4sim-approvals.md`; sources read as full text at arXiv on 2026-11-07, quotes in `benchmark-facts.json` under `.g4sim-work`, not read in journal form):

| Benchmark | Geometry and beam | Quantity and threshold | Negative control, which must fail the same threshold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calorimeter linearity, ScECAL, arXiv:1707.07126 | 30 x (3.49 mm WC of the balanced composition, 14.25 g/cm3; 3.02 mm polystyrene; 2.36 mm air), 180 mm side; e- at 2, 4, 8, 12, 15, 20, 30, 32 GeV | Active energy against beam energy, linear fit with free slope and offset: largest deviation at most 1.5%. Published maximum (1.1 +- 0.4)%; the deviations derived from its table reach -1.46% | 10 layers instead of 30 |
| Calorimeter resolution, AHCAL, arXiv:1012.4343 and 1003.2662 | 38 x (21.4 mm S235 steel; 5.0 mm polystyrene; 5.23 mm support of FR4, foil, cable and air), 900 mm side; e+ at 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50 GeV | RMS over mean of the active energy, fit a/sqrt(E) (+) b: a within 21.9 +- 2.8 %sqrt(GeV), on both `FTFP_BERT_EMZ` and `FTFP_BERT_EMY` | Steel doubled |
| Thin-silicon scattering, Berger, arXiv:1405.2759 | One 50 um Si wafer, or two for 100 um, in vacuum, normal incidence; e- at 1, 2, 3 GeV/c | RMS of the central 98% of the planar angle, per row: within 2 sigma of the data, sigma = statistical (+) 5%; 6 rows | A 100 um run judged against the 50 um row |
| Material budget, CMS module, arXiv:2407.13721 | 200 um Si plane | x/X0 of the plane through a geantino scan: 0.214% within 0.005% | The plane removed |

Labelled checks, not references: `sampling-fraction` and `shower-containment` (sanity), `reco-closure` (verification, nominal Gaussian errors, tracker residuals against momentum in a field, thresholds of S4's `reco_from_hits.py`), `tof-beta` (verification: flight time between TOF planes against L/(beta c) within 0.1%, control: plane gap doubled in the expectation).

Events: 2,000 per point for linearity, resolution and scattering, about 8 core-hours from a recorded trial; the maintainer approved the run ("approve the benchmark run"). The material benchmark is one geantino path and needs no sample.

The ScECAL support layer is air by assumption: the paper gives about 67 mm of readout and gaps no thickness. The AHCAL 0.5 MIP cell cut and noise term c = 58 MeV are not simulated, so a is fitted without c. Gap accepted by the maintainer: TOF time resolution, transverse shape, hit efficiency, beam-test sampling fraction, tracker residuals against data, longitudinal shape as a weak band. A model-confirmation record compares the EM physics of the two lists read back from the run, and refuses `FTFP_BERT_EMZ` with `QGSP_BIC_EMZ` for electrons.
