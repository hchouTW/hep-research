# g4sim phase S8: Scale and conditional items

## Background

Phase S8 of the g4sim plan (Part B of `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task.md`, Section 19.2). Labels S0-S9, T1, T2 and GB1-GB24 are local to that plan (Section 1).

This card uses the headings `lint_task.py` requires, and carries the eight sections of Section E5, in E5's order, as level-4 headings under them: Goal, Scope checklist, Deliverables, To-verify list, Thresholds and test designs, Environments, Risks and stop points, Execution log.

Pre-registration (Section 19.2): this card is fixed by its SHA-256 in `docs/dev/geant4-architecture/g4sim-task-registry.json` before any run a threshold judges. Thresholds may be tightened, never loosened, except through the falsification procedure of Section 23, as addendum 11.1 did for gate 3. An entry that only appends to the Execution log is registered again with that reason, so the thresholds keep their earlier registration.

## Objective

#### Goal

Deliver logical-level checks, the run card's `placement_impl`, fast-simulation hooks, and the conditional items `g4sim_digi` and GDML import only if their conditions hold (Section 19.2, S8).

## Scope

### In Scope

#### Scope checklist

**Content (Section 19.2)**

- [ ] logical-level checks; the run card's `placement_impl` (assembly).
- [ ] Fast-simulation hooks: the `fast_sim_envelope` envelope and the `G4VFastSimulationModel` interface. This plan delivers only the hooks; the substitute models themselves are validated separately, under hep-ml's protocol, by the studies that use them, and are not part of this plan's gates.
- [ ] Conditional items:
  - [ ] `g4sim_digi`: built only if Python cannot meet the throughput budget.
  - [ ] GDML import: built only if a reference case needs it and the installation has the GDML feature.

**Versions (Sections 15.5, 19.2)**

- [ ] minor versions of core, contracts (the `fast_sim_envelope` requirement; IR minor version) and adapters/geant4 (the run card's `placement_impl`); plugin minor version. Adapter numbers are proposed values, assigned in merge order at merge time.

**Gates (Section 19.2)**: the text of each gate is under Thresholds and test designs.

- [ ] Every gate of this phase is recorded with evidence in the gate record.
- [ ] Common checks (Section 19.2; `geant4-program.md` §A.8): `run_all_checks.py`, `validate.py --strict` and `dependencies.py` on every study record, `check_wiring.py`, `check_layering.py`, and the full existing test suite.

**This phase's negative tests (Section 15.1)**

- [ ] Specification: logical-level checks; `placement_impl`; the `fast_sim_envelope` requirement. Negative test: Two logicals sharing a name; specifying `replica` or `parameterised`, which addendum 11.1 removes; `assembly` for a placement whose template has daughters of its own, because the imprint removes that template's level from the copy chain; a daughter volume inside the envelope

**Items of Section 22**

- [ ] Re-evaluation of qualification at every phase's release (type: Approval; responsible: Custodian; when: At every release from S0 to S9)

### Out of Scope

- Substitute fast-simulation models, validated separately under hep-ml's protocol.
- `g4sim_digi` unless Python misses the throughput budget.
- GDML import unless a reference case needs it and the installation has the feature.

#### Thresholds and test designs

**Gates, verbatim from Section 19.2**

1. Within the 10⁶-touchable numbers of the performance budget document.
2. If `g4sim_digi` is built: judged by the determinism table of Section 10.2, continuous values are within the declared ulp tolerance; every discrete disagreement, traced along data dependencies, has as its root a draw within a decision boundary; the number of affected sub-streams is reported.
3. A run with no `fast_sim_envelope` attaches no fast-simulation process. With a fast-simulation model attached that never triggers, the mean deposit of each observable is equivalent to having no hook, not bit-identical: with the same seeds, a paired TOST on the per-event differences of each observable, grouped as in S3 gate 3, α = 0.05, margin ln 1.005 of the mean, passing only if all pass, with the number of events and the power derived in the S8 task card under Section 18. In the S3 prototype, with the process attached to all particles, `G4FastSimulationManagerProcess` changed about 1% of histories (GB19); with a test model attached that kills particles at the envelope entrance and books their energy as deposit, the total deposit inside the envelope equals the incident energy (tolerance as in the closure check of Section 9.1).
4. This phase's negative tests.
5. G5 confirmation of the fast-simulation hooks, the builder of `placement_impl` and (if built) `g4sim_digi`.

**Phase-specific values and rules**

- Gate 1: the 10⁶-touchable numbers of the performance budget of S1. Registered design (`s8_gates/scale.py`): check-spec (`contracts/geometry.check_file`) on two specifications of 10⁶ touchables, nested (10³ modules of 10³ cells, 2,000 IR placements) and flat (10⁶ cells in one mother), each at most 300 s, timed once on a Slurm node. Peak memory is reported, not judged: the budget sets no limit on it.
- Gate 2 (`g4sim_digi` is built): `s8_gates/digi_determinism.py` with 2,000 events of bins and seed 20261109, two fixtures: (A) analytic efficiencies, the Gamma yield variation and photon times with rise and decay; (B) mapped arrival times and a dark-count mean of 50 (PTRS). Level 1: the first 1,000 output groups of three sub-streams identical. Level 2: shuffled bins and three chunks by event byte-identical. Level 3: photoelectrons, ADC codes and the number of times identical; declared tolerances, before any comparison: charge at most 8 ulp (a Gaussian-derived value, `rng.CROSS_PLATFORM_ULPS["gaussian"]`), photon time at most 8 ulp; a discrete disagreement must trace to a draw within 2⁻⁴⁰ of a decision boundary, and affected sub-streams are counted. The fixtures must reach PTRS, BTPE, Gamma and both inverse transforms.
- Gate 3, part 1 (paired equivalence with a never-triggering model, addendum 11.1), `s8_gates/hook_equivalence.py`, two-step registration: S3 gate 3's ZDC prototype v2 (e+ at 500 MeV aimed at crystal (4, 2), spot 4.4 mm, FTFP_BERT_EMZ, cut 0.7 mm, `separated`), its crystal template as the fast_sim_envelope (the only template of `CrystalRegion`), the idle model on e-, e+ and gamma; no hook against the idle hook with the same seeds. Grouping as S3 gate 3: a crystal whose no-hook mean is at least 1% of the beam is its own observable, the rest are summed. For each observable, the log ratio of means and the delta-method variance of w = y/ȳ − x/x̄ per event; TOST at α = 0.05, margin ln 1.005, all K must pass; each test's power 0.9^(1/K) at a true difference of 0, joint power 0.9; events n = (z₀.₉₅ + z₍₁₊ₚ₎/₂)² Var(w) / margin², the largest over the observables, and at least 2,000. Step 1 (this registration): an independent trial of 20,000 paired events, seed 940001; step 2 registers its K, grouping, Var(w) and the events, and the test runs at seed 940002. The read-back requires the process `fastSimProcess_massGeom` on exactly e-, e+ and gamma in the hooked run and on no particle in the run with no hook.
- Gate 3, part 2 (closure): an envelope of PbWO4 (400 mm cube) alone in vacuum, the absorb model; beams of e-, e+ and gamma at 1 GeV and protons at 5 GeV, 1,000 events each, seed 940003. Every event's deposit inside the envelope equals the incident kinetic energy within Section 9.1's n 2⁻⁵² Σ|xᵢ| with n = 1 (the model books one deposit), every event is scored, and the conservation identity holds within 1e-6 MeV.
- Gate 4: `s8_gates/negatives.py`, the four rows of Section 15.1 for S8 as code changes, each caught by its named test.
- Gate 5: G5 of `g4sim_run/FastSim.hh`, the assembly builder in `g4sim_run/NewBuild.hh`, the S8 changes of `g4sim_run/NewRun.cc` and `g4sim/Accounting.hh`, and `g4sim_digi/` (`Samplers.hh`, `g4sim_digi.cc`), after an independent read.
- Not judged, recorded: the S7 reference cases rerun on the S8 build, to show that runs without the S8 features are unchanged.
- Rule: if `g4sim_digi` is built, the ulp tolerance is declared here before comparison (Section 10.2): declared above.
