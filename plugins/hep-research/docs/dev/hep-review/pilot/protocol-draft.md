# G2 narrow pilot: EvaluationProtocol draft (T03 step 7; not frozen, nothing scored)

Spec v9 sections 15.2-15.5. This draft is frozen only at the start of T04, after the user gate U1. Nothing in it is a
result. The T0S spike instances (`../spike/instances/`) are exposed and excluded (case N25).

## 1. Suite

| Item | Draft value |
|---|---|
| Domain / method / format | statistics (uncertainty model, propagation with correlations) with a collaborating computing obligation (recompute within 0.1%); text + JSON |
| Types | C01 known non-cosmetic defect, C02 correct same-scope control, C03 missing required input, C04 intent minimum requirement left unplanned |
| Instances | 4 per type, **new**: generated by `make_pilot_instances.py --seed <secret>` from three families not used in the spike (weights 1/sigma, binomial efficiency, product of anticorrelated quantities) plus a C03 family (a missing per-measurement sigma) |
| Oracle | deterministic, per instance, run before writing it: C01 the stated value follows the defective formula and differs from the correct one beyond tolerance; C02 correct at the stated precision (quoted with 6 significant digits); C03 stated values reachable for the true missing input and not determinable without it; C04 material correct and REQ-2 unmapped |
| Gold | `expected/<id>.json` + `oracle.json` written by the generator, separate from `instances/` |
| Trials | 3 per instance (48 trials), each a fresh-context reviewer, full R2 path (snapshot, plan, assignment, packet, drop file, ingest, commit, report); no retry; every trial kept |
| Budget | at most 60 reviewer dispatches including grader calibration; stop and report on exhaustion |

## 2. Success conditions (spec 15.5), stated per trial

- C01: `violated` on the defective obligation with a finding naming the element and a reason matching the oracle's
  defect (graded; see 4).
- C02: `supported` on both obligations, no finding on either; a well-founded remark under missing_inputs is allowed.
- C03: the obligation needing the missing input is `undetermined` or `not-examined` with that input named.
- C04: the projection gives `plan_requirements_met = false` with REQ-2 a planning gap (mechanical).

Engineering gate (separate): every trial lands a drop file that ingests by bytes; fidelity file-transfer or better;
the report is recomputed byte-for-byte from an export; head continuity verified; no quarantine except planned ones.

Scientific gate (separate), on all 48 planned trials (timeouts and invalid submissions count as failures): C01 correct
with evidence in at least 10 of 12 trials and in at least 3 of 4 instances; C02 false alarms in at most 2 of 12; C03 gap
kept in at least 10 of 12; C04 mechanical 12 of 12. Report first-attempt, at-least-once and all-scheduled success per
instance; never a power of the single-run rate.

## 3. Denominators (spec 15.3)

Task level (16 instances: planned, started, completed, successful trials; not started, timeout, tool error, invalid
submission, abstention, unscorable), known-defect level (each C01 defect per trial, counted at most once however many
findings it is split into), predicted-finding level (deduplicated claim groups: verified correct, verified wrong,
unadjudicated, with the unadjudicated share shown).

## 4. Grading and calibration

The C02, C03 and C04 checks and the "violated on the right obligation" part of C01 are mechanical against the oracle.
The C01 reason match needs a grader: a fresh-context model grader with the oracle's defect statement and the finding,
calibrated before scoring on 8 items (2 each: defect present, correct same-scope control, insufficient information,
correct but differently worded solution) with thresholds fixed in advance (8/8 required; an always-agree or
always-reject grader fails by construction), plus order-swap and length-change probes as bias checks only. Without
calibration the C01 evidence part is labelled uncalibrated and the scientific gate is not passed.

## 5. Salted commitments and custody (spec 15.4)

Before the first trial, for every `expected/<id>.json` and the grader rubric: `evaluation.commit_gold(bytes)` gives a
32-byte nonce and `SHA-256(nonce || bytes)`; the commitments are written to the evaluation log (a separate store
context). On this host reviewers share the filesystem (host profile H1), so nothing stored here is held out:

| Option (U1) | Who holds gold and nonces | What Claude sees | What can be claimed |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | the user, off the host; the user runs the generator with a private seed and the private check | the instances only (copied in by the user), the commitments, the published check results | held-out isolation: met (declared by the user) |
| (b) | nobody needs secret gold: the user approves the deterministic oracles; Claude generates with a seed the user chooses | everything, including the oracle verdicts | held-out isolation: unmet; scores are limited to "oracle-checked, not held out" |
| (c) | stop at G1 | - | G1 only |

The nonce is published only after every planned trial using that gold has finished; a nonce published early makes the
later trials no longer held-out. A gold/commitment mismatch makes that batch unscorable (outputs kept).

## 6. Starting context and environment per trial

Fresh subagent context (Agent tool), the packet text of `coordinator.packet_text`, only snapshot paths as material,
drop path per assignment, model and host as recorded in the host profile (model alias not pinned: record the date),
tools as the host provides them (advisory restrictions only).
