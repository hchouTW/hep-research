# Goodness of fit of a linear calibration

Fifteen calibration points y_i with uncertainties sigma_i are fitted by weighted least squares with a straight line
f(x) = a + b x (two free parameters). The goodness of fit is quoted as chi-square / ndf with ndf = 15 - 2 = 13, and the
p-value is the chi-square survival function at ndf = 13.
