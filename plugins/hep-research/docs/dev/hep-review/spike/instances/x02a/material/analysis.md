# Combination of two branching-fraction measurements

Two measurements of the same branching fraction are combined with BLUE. Each has a statistical and a systematic
uncertainty; the systematic uncertainties share a common efficiency model and have correlation coefficient 0.5
between the two measurements (statistical uncertainties are independent). The covariance used, the weights
w = V^-1 1 / (1^T V^-1 1) and the combined uncertainty (1^T V^-1 1)^-1/2 are in data.json.
