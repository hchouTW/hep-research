# Reference consolidation proposals

Status: **P1 executed in a modified form on 2026-10-13** (hep-computing remediation CMP-25, maintainer decision Q1;
see below); the other proposals are not executed. Every number here comes from
[reference-inventory.md](reference-inventory.md) (generated by `tools/reference_inventory.py`; `--check` in
`tools/run_all_checks.py` fails when it goes stale). The maintainer decided on 2026-10-03 to keep the non-physics
reference material; on 2026-10-13 that decision was revised (Q1 of the hep-computing remediation): consolidate the
design guidelines (P1), move only the web, sprint, feature-flag and accessibility material and `product-framing.md`
(part of P2), and keep the task-authoring scripts and the prompt-engineering, loop-engineering and agile references.

## Executed

- **P1 (2026-10-13, CMP-25).** Instead of a shared `balanced-design-core.md`, the four design-guideline files were
  cut to their own subjects: `root-balanced-design-guidelines.md` keeps only ROOT-specific rules and the conventions
  every hep-computing reference shares (naming, the C++17 baseline, errors, paths, non-owning members, the comment
  rule); `cpp-`, `python-` and `bash-balanced-design-guidelines.md` became checklists around HEP failure modes with
  grep-able anti-pattern tables. Toy examples (`BankAccount`, `Animal`, `Shape`, `MathHelper`, `ReportGenerator`)
  were removed. The four files went from 4,573 to about 1,030 lines; file names and the anchors other skills link
  to are unchanged.
- **P2, in part (2026-10-13, CMP-26).** `product-framing.md` moved (with `git mv`) to
  [dev/engineering/product-framing.md](dev/engineering/product-framing.md); the playbook's New Endpoint, UI Feature,
  Database Migration and Incident Response sections, the Sprint planning and Feature flags sections of
  `design-and-estimation.md` and the Accessibility section of `risk-and-quality.md` moved to
  [dev/engineering/web-and-delivery-practices.md](dev/engineering/web-and-delivery-practices.md). Each old place keeps
  a one-line pointer; the skill's links point to the new paths. Kept in the skill: `agile-development-guide.md`
  (cited by physics-ml), `prompt-engineering-and-token-optimization.md` and `loop-engineering.md` (required by
  `scripts/validate_skill_bundle.py` and the task guide), and the six task- and example-authoring scripts (CMP-27
  deferred).

## What the inventory shows

- 169 references in 7 skills, 1.38 MB; every reference is cited by at least one plugin file (no orphans).
- Textual overlap is low: only three pairs reach the listing thresholds (Jaccard >= 0.08 or 15% of the smaller file's
  8-word shingles found in the other). Similar names mostly mark different topics, not duplicates; the
  hypothesis of the review (overlap among similarly named references) holds for one family only.
- `last_commit` comes from a shallow clone, so most files show the oldest available commit (2026-10-04), not their
  last meaningful change.
